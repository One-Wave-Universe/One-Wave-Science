# NO ENTANGLEMENT

This is a One Wave rule, not a mood.

There is **no entanglement** in this science.

## What happens instead

A wave hits a **detector** or a **lens in the eye**.
At that **when**, the cut is **plus or minus** (peak or trough, field or void).

A brain or a computer can store those signs along the path and **map peaks and troughs back**:

- how many fences were crossed
- in what order
- with what delay

From that record you get **distance and placement**. Not from a twin that "knows" without a net.

Where is a label on a bench. When-plus-sign is the measurement.

## Connection

Two things affect each other only if they share a connection you can point at:

- a wire
- a CENTER / (0) node
- a current
- a path the wave actually ran
- a receipt / packet that was passed

If you cannot name the net, it is not in the circuit.

Mirrors are opposite gates on one loop.
Hexes are wired neighbors.
Field and void are polarity on that loop.

## Reject

- spooky pairing
- remote collapse-as-control
- "the other side knows without a net"
- using the word entanglement to skip a detector

One wave. Local connection. Choice at a fence. Hold on the magnet.
Eye and meter are detectors. They read + or −. Mapping is memory of those cuts.

Falsifier: a claimed effect with no named net, no detector cut, no receipt. Rejected here.
