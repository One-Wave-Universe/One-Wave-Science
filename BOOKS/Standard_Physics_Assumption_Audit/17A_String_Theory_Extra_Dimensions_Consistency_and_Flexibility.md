## 1. The claim to audit

A common criticism says:

> String theory keeps adding dimensions whenever the theory needs saving.

That is too crude.

In critical string theories, the number of spacetime dimensions is not simply chosen because four dimensions failed. It emerges from consistency conditions in the quantized worldsheet theory, including cancellation of anomalies.

However, a separate criticism remains serious:

> Once extra dimensions are accepted, the enormous freedom in how they are compactified and how fields/fluxes are arranged can produce many possible low-energy effective theories, making unique empirical prediction difficult.

These are different claims and must not be blended.

## 2. Bosonic string critical dimension

For the bosonic string, the worldsheet matter sector contributes central charge

$$
c_{\mathrm{matter}}=D,
$$

where $D$ is spacetime dimension.

The reparameterization ghost sector contributes

$$
c_{\mathrm{ghost}}=-26.
$$

Conformal anomaly cancellation requires

$$
c_{\mathrm{total}}
=c_{\mathrm{matter}}+c_{\mathrm{ghost}}
=0.
$$

Therefore

$$
D-26=0
$$

and

$$
\boxed{D=26}.
$$

That is a consistency requirement of the critical bosonic string, not a dimension added to patch one failed astronomical observation.

## 3. Superstring critical dimension

In the Ramond-Neveu-Schwarz formulation of the superstring, the matter contribution includes bosonic coordinates and worldsheet fermions. Schematically,

$$
c_{\mathrm{matter}}
=D+\frac{D}{2}
=\frac{3D}{2}.
$$

The superconformal ghost sectors contribute total

$$
c_{\mathrm{ghost}}=-15.
$$

Anomaly cancellation requires

$$
\frac{3D}{2}-15=0,
$$

so

$$
\boxed{D=10}.
$$

Again, ten dimensions arise from consistency of the critical formulation.

## 4. Why eleven dimensions appear

The five consistent ten-dimensional superstring theories were later understood as related by dualities. Strong-coupling limits point toward an eleven-dimensional framework commonly called M-theory.

The important audit point is:

**the move from ten-dimensional string theories to an eleven-dimensional unifying framework was not simply "add a dimension because the old prediction failed."**

It arose from mathematical relations among theories and their strong-coupling behavior.

But mathematical unification is not the same thing as experimental confirmation.

## 5. Compactification creates the real flexibility problem

Observed macroscopic physics appears approximately four-dimensional.

A higher-dimensional theory therefore needs some mechanism making the additional dimensions inaccessible at ordinary scales, commonly through compactification.

A simple product picture is

$$
\mathcal M_{10}
\sim
\mathcal M_{3,1}\times K_6,
$$

where $\mathcal M_{3,1}$ is four-dimensional spacetime and $K_6$ is a compact six-dimensional internal space.

Different choices of

- compactification geometry,
- topology,
- fluxes,
- branes,
- moduli stabilization,
- symmetry breaking,
- vacuum state

can produce different four-dimensional effective theories.

Schematically,

$$
\{K,\,F,\,\text{branes},\,\text{moduli},\ldots\}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal L_{\mathrm{eff}}^{(4D)}.
$$

This is where underdetermination becomes a central audit issue.

## 6. Internal consistency is not empirical uniqueness

A theory can satisfy stringent mathematical consistency constraints while still allowing many phenomenologically distinct solutions.

Therefore two questions must be kept separate:

### Consistency question

Does the theory avoid internal contradiction/anomaly?

### Selection question

Why does our observed universe correspond to this particular vacuum/compactification/effective theory rather than another allowed one?

If a framework admits a very large landscape of possibilities, a successful fit after choosing among them is weaker than a sharp prediction made before the observation.

## 7. The anti-reverse-fitting test

Suppose a compactification/model is specified by parameters or discrete choices

$$
\theta=(\theta_1,\theta_2,\ldots,\theta_n).
$$

It predicts observables

$$
\hat O_i=F_i(\theta).
$$

If $\theta$ is selected only after observing

$$
O_i^{\mathrm{obs}},
$$

then agreement

$$
F_i(\theta)\approx O_i^{\mathrm{obs}}
$$

is a fit, not automatically a prediction.

The framework becomes stronger when the selected structure yields additional quantities

$$
O_j^{\mathrm{new}}
$$

that were not used to choose $\theta$ and are later verified.

## 8. What this book should criticize

VALID CRITICISM:
- lack of unique low-energy predictions from a huge space of compactifications/vacua;
- parameters or vacuum choices selected after known data;
- explanations that turn mathematically allowed structures into experimentally established objects;
- weak falsifiability caused by too much model flexibility.

INVALID CARICATURE:
- "string theorists randomly add a dimension every time something breaks."

The critical dimensions themselves arise from nontrivial consistency conditions.

## 9. AI changes this audit

Historically, evaluating these arguments required specialized command of conformal field theory, topology, supersymmetry, compactification, and quantum field theory.

AI-assisted mathematics can increasingly help outsiders:

- reconstruct anomaly-cancellation calculations;
- check assumptions;
- enumerate dependencies;
- compare compactifications;
- test low-energy consequences;
- distinguish fitted from predicted quantities;
- formalize parts of the mathematics.

That does not let AI decide physical truth by eloquence.

It lets more people ask whether the chain

$$
\text{assumption}
\rightarrow
\text{math}
\rightarrow
\text{prediction}
\rightarrow
\text{measurement}
$$

actually closes.

## 10. Kill tests / promotion tests

A string-derived model becomes scientifically stronger when it provides:

1. a clearly specified compactification/vacuum;
2. independently fixed parameters;
3. Standard Model-like low-energy content;
4. quantitative predictions not used in model selection;
5. experimentally accessible distinguishing signatures;
6. failure conditions that exclude broad regions of the model space.

A framework becomes weaker as an explanation if essentially any observation can be accommodated by moving to another allowed construction after the fact.
