### Executed conversion control

The final command uses **declared test inputs**, not a CERN event:
E = 1 GeV and separately p = 1 GeV/c. With the chart's SI constants,

```text
f_E = E/h             = 2.417989242084918e23 Hz
lambda_photon = hc/E  = 1.2398419843320027e-15 m
lambda_p = h/p        = 1.2398419843320027e-15 m
omega_E = 2*pi*f_E    (angular frequency; do not confuse it with f_E)
```

The two wavelengths coincide for these chosen numbers; that does not authorize
setting E=pc for a massive excitation. For an invariant mass M, Mc²/h is a
rest-energy frequency coordinate; laboratory total energy gives a different,
frame-dependent coordinate. Preserve the provider's mass/energy definition.

For a linear energy-frequency transform, sigma_f = sigma_E/h and
Cov(f_i,f_j) = Cov(E_i,E_j)/h². For a density histogram with E=h f,
dN/df = h dN/dE when E is in joules (use the eV conversion factor otherwise);
bin counts themselves remain unchanged. A comparison must transform bin edges,
units and covariance consistently. None of these transforms manufactures phase.
