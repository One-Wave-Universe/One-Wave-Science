# CHAPTER 7 — SCHRÖDINGER'S CAT: THOUGHT EXPERIMENT, NOT MEASUREMENT

Status: YELLOW — interpretation audit; no One-Wave replacement claimed

CORE-RULES-PRE:
- Preserve the quantum mathematics and the historical purpose of the thought experiment.
- Separate measured facts from interpretation.
- Do not turn a criticism of interpretation into a claim that microscopic superposition is experimentally false.
- Relevant rules: 1, 2, 3, 7, 9, 10, 12, 15, 16, 17, 18.

## 1. What the cat actually is

Schrödinger's cat is a thought experiment designed to expose the tension between microscopic quantum superposition and definite macroscopic outcomes.

It is not an experiment in which physicists place a real cat in a coherent alive/dead state and then discover that looking at it changes reality.

## 2. Accepted mathematical setup

Let a microscopic trigger have two basis states:

$$
|u\rangle = |\text{undecayed}\rangle,
\qquad
|d\rangle = |\text{decayed}\rangle.
$$

Before coupling to the macroscopic apparatus, a simplified state can be written

$$
|\psi\rangle
=
\alpha |u\rangle + \beta |d\rangle,
\qquad
|\alpha|^2+|\beta|^2=1.
$$

Couple the trigger to an apparatus/cat state:

$$
|u\rangle|A_0\rangle
\rightarrow
|u\rangle|\text{alive}\rangle,
$$

$$
|d\rangle|A_0\rangle
\rightarrow
|d\rangle|\text{dead}\rangle.
$$

By linearity,

$$
(\alpha|u\rangle+\beta|d\rangle)|A_0\rangle
\rightarrow
\alpha|u\rangle|\text{alive}\rangle
+
\beta|d\rangle|\text{dead}\rangle.
$$

That is the paradoxical state used to expose the measurement problem.

## 3. Where decoherence enters

Once the macroscopic alternatives couple differently to an environment,

$$
|\Psi\rangle
=
\alpha|u\rangle|\text{alive}\rangle|E_a\rangle
+
\beta|d\rangle|\text{dead}\rangle|E_d\rangle.
$$

The reduced density matrix of the trigger+cat system contains off-diagonal terms weighted by

$$
\langle E_d|E_a\rangle.
$$

For macroscopically distinguishable environmental records,

$$
|\langle E_d|E_a\rangle|\ll1,
$$

so local interference between the macroscopic alternatives becomes effectively inaccessible.

This explains suppression of observable coherence. Depending on interpretation, it does not by itself settle why one definite record is experienced in one run.

## 4. What popular language gets wrong

Bad shorthand:

> The cat is literally both dead and alive until a human looks.

Better audit statement:

> Linear quantum evolution applied without an additional outcome rule produces a correlated superposition of macroscopically distinct alternatives; Schrödinger used the cat to make the resulting interpretive problem impossible to ignore.

The thought experiment is therefore evidence of a conceptual tension in the formalism/interpretation boundary, not direct experimental evidence for mystical consciousness-driven reality.

## 5. What is actually experimentally established

Coherent superpositions and interference have been demonstrated in microscopic and increasingly mesoscopic systems. Those results constrain any replacement theory.

The cat story itself is not the measurement.

The required target is the observed interference, decoherence, outcome probabilities, and macroscopic records.

## 6. One-Wave audit

One-Wave may propose that an extended wave physically couples into a detector/environment and produces a stable macroscopic record without consciousness.

But that proposal must derive:

$$
P_i = |c_i|^2
$$

or an experimentally equivalent outcome law, plus the disappearance of observable interference for macroscopic records.

It is not enough to say "the cat was always one thing." That would merely assert an ontology unless the detector and probability mathematics reproduce the data.

## 7. Kill tests

A One-Wave account fails or must narrow if it cannot reproduce:

1. microscopic interference;
2. controlled decoherence rates;
3. recovery of coherence where experimentally possible;
4. Born-rule outcome frequencies;
5. macroscopic record stability;
6. Bell/CHSH correlations for entangled systems.

## 8. Audit result

PASS:
- Schrödinger's cat is a thought experiment about the measurement problem.
- Conscious observation is not required to write the physical system-apparatus interaction.

YELLOW:
- Which ontology best explains definite outcomes remains interpretation-dependent unless a replacement dynamics is derived and experimentally distinguished.
- One-Wave has not yet derived the complete outcome law.

- No consciousness mechanism inserted.
- One-Wave not promoted without probability and detector derivation.
