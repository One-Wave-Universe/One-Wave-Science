Circle of Fifths · Harmonic Butterfly

Three note rings align on a single radial tip: outer −5, middle root (0), inner +4 major / +3 minor. Each selected played note gets a separate moving-line butterfly.

Calculated frequency and crossing times

12-tone equal temperament, A4=440 Hz. Adjacent zero crossings = 500/f milliseconds for a steady sine wave. Equal-tempered fifth ratio = 2^(7/12), not exactly 3/2. Interference is linear summation; energy transfer needs a measured coupling model.
