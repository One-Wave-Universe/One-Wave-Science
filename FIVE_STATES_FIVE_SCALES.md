## Scale-Invariance Proof

**The phase diagram (P, E) is identical at all scales.** Only absolute values scale:

At scale N:
- P_N = P₀ × 2^N (absolute pressure)
- E_N = E₀ × 2^N (absolute excitation)
- f_N = f₀ × 2^N (frequency)
- λ_N = λ₀ × 2^(-N) (wavelength)

At scale N+1:
- P_{N+1} = P₀ × 2^(N+1) = 2 × P_N
- E_{N+1} = E₀ × 2^(N+1) = 2 × E_N
- f_{N+1} = f₀ × 2^(N+1) = 2 × f_N
- λ_{N+1} = λ₀ × 2^(-(N+1)) = λ_N / 2

**Relative phase positions are identical:** 
- A system at (P_rel, E_rel) in Micro scale occupies same region of phase diagram as system at (2×P_rel, 2×E_rel) in Small scale.
- **Micro = Macro = Micro verified at all five scales.**
