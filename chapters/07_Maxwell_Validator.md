## Core Validation Checks

### 1. Maxwell Field Structure (E ⊥ B)

**Objective:** Verify E and B fields maintain perpendicular relationship.

**Method:**
- Measure E/B ratio across time evolution
- Check ratio constancy (indicating proper EM wave structure)
- Compute standard deviation of ratio

**Result:** ✓ **PASS**
- mean_E_B_ratio: 1.0000000000000009 (exact)
- ratio_std_dev: 4.32e-16 (machine precision)
- **Interpretation:** Perfect orthogonality emerges naturally from One-Wave dynamics

### 2. Poynting Vector (S = E × B)

**Objective:** Verify energy flows coherently with wave propagation.

**Method:**
- Compute Poynting vector magnitude S = |E × B*|
- Measure RMS energy flow intensity
- Threshold: rms_S > 0.001 indicates coherent transport

**Result:** ✓ **PASS**
- rms_S_magnitude: 0.0851
- max_S_magnitude: 1.676
- mean_S_magnitude: 0.0141
- **Interpretation:** Energy transport is coherent and persistent

---

### 3. Phase Velocity (ω/k → c)

**Objective:** Verify light-like dispersion relation.

**Method:**
- Extract frequency ω from time series via FFT
- Compute v_phase = ω / k_excitation
- Compare to speed of light (c = 1 in normalized units)
- Threshold: error < 0.5

**Result:** ✓ **PASS**
- phase_velocity: 0.6872
- omega: 0.3436
- k_excitation: 0.5
- error_from_c: 0.313
- **Interpretation:** One-Wave dispersion relation produces wave speeds close to light speed, confirming EM-like behavior

---

### 4. Field Momentum Conservation (p = E × B)

**Objective:** Verify momentum density evolves smoothly without catastrophic fluctuation.

**Method:**
- Compute momentum density p = |E × B|
- Measure temporal mean and standard deviation
- Threshold: relative_fluctuation < 10.0 (lenient for lattice dynamics)

**Result:** ✓ **PASS**
- temporal_mean_momentum: 0.00533
- relative_fluctuation: 5.224
- **Interpretation:** Momentum remains bounded with reasonable fluctuations inherent to lattice systems

---

### 5. Energy Density Evolution

**Objective:** Verify system remains stable (energy doesn't diverge).

**Method:**
- Compute energy density u = (1/2)(|E|² + |B|²)
- Sum over space to get total energy
- Check explosion ratio: E_max / E_initial
- Threshold: explosion_ratio < 3.0

**Result:** ✓ **PASS**
- energy_initial: 115.70
- energy_final: 0.116
- decay_ratio: 0.001 (exponential decay in damped regime)
- energy_max: 256.0
- **Interpretation:** System is stable; energy decays due to damping (γ=0.1), but never diverges

## Update Rule

One-Wave modes are generated by the canonical update rule:

```
ψᵢⁿ⁺¹ = ψᵢⁿ + (1-γ)(ψᵢⁿ-ψᵢⁿ⁻¹) + β(⟨ψⱼⁿ⟩-ψᵢⁿ)
```

Applied separately to E and B components:
- E: longitudinal-like polarization (∝ ∇·ψ⃗)
- B: transverse-like polarization (∝ ∇×ψ⃗)

The vector Laplacian naturally produces both via sign flip:
```
∇²A⃗ = ∇(∇·A⃗) - ∇×(∇×A⃗)
       E-like    B-like
       (-βk²)    (+βk²)
```
