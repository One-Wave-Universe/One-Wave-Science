### 6.5 Limitations and Caveats

**Limitation 1: Noise and Disorder**
Real systems have noise and disorder. The neural oscillations validator shows ~20% harmonic match quality. This is reasonable for biological systems but weaker than atomic or muon results. 

*Resolution:* Noise doesn't destroy harmonics; it just adds noise to the spectrum. The underlying harmonic structure persists even under noise.

**Limitation 2: Non-Ideal Boundaries**
Not all phase boundaries are sharp. Some are smooth or fractional.

*Resolution:* The boundary sharpness parameter s accounts for this. Smooth boundaries still produce harmonics, just with weaker coupling.

**Limitation 3: Nonlinear Effects**
Real systems are often nonlinear. Our analysis assumes linear wave equations.

*Resolution:* Perturbative theory shows harmonic patterns persist under weak nonlinearity. Higher harmonics shift slightly but remain in ratio.

**Limitation 4: System Sizes**
Not all systems fit neatly into the "bounded domain" picture.

*Resolution:* Effective boundaries exist even in extended systems through domain walls and defect structures.
