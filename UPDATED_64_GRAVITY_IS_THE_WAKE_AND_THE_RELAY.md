**Date:** 2026-10-05

## What gravity is

Gravity is the wake and the relay system. One thing.

The wake is the displaced medium a parent creates. The relay is that displacement passed, site to site, as the bodies move. There is no separate force riding on the wake, and there is no second system whose job is to carry a message about the wake.

Where the slope is still distinguishable from the reference, the wake is relaying. Where it is not, gravity has ended. Nothing is stored.

## Parent and child

The parent creates the wake. The child does not.

The child rides the wake the parent made and adds its own displacement and its own motion to the party. That changes the parent. It does not start a second wake.

A child whose own slope is no longer distinguishable has nothing of its own left to add. Until then it is a member of the party, not a passenger and not a second well.

## Many wakes

A body is caught in every wake that is still distinguishable where it sits.

The Moon rides the wake the Earth created. The Earth and the Moon together ride the wake the Sun created. Jupiter creates its own wake. On the Newtonian contrast, Jupiter's acceleration at the Moon is on the order of 10⁻⁴ of the Earth's acceleration at the Moon, so the slope is small and it is not zero. If that slope is still distinguishable from the reference, the Moon is in Jupiter's wake too. Saturn and the rest join on the same rule, smaller again.

Each of those parents created the wake the Moon is caught in. The Moon adds its displacement and its motion into the shared party. It does not start those wakes. A wake that has fallen into the reference has already ended.

## What the older "no relay" lines meant

Those lines are not a denial that gravity is the relay. Gravity is the passing of the present slope. "No memory relay" means no stored memory of a pull that has already ended.
