# DC · AC · RC — three axes, one sphere

```
DC     X     opposed clothes     + | 0 | −     hold / engage
AC     Y     crossing            + ↔ 0 ↔ −     lean / walk
RC     Z     window              delay / tau    when you stamp
```

A point on the sphere is one flip: where the clothes sit (DC), which way the live winding leans (AC), how far through the window you are (RC).

Walk A→B→C is rotation in the AC plane. STAY is a latitude: RC runs, DC holds, AC amplitude ~0. Reinjection is staying on the sphere instead of dropping to the origin and climbing again.

Hex slice = AC walk in the plane. Cube stack = DC clothes copied in Z. Sphere = those plus RC so the path has duration, not just a drawing.

Same three lines at every cell. No fourth axis.
