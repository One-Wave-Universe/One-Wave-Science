# CELL_V1 Full Physical Build — Three Rails, Three Bidirectional Mirror Stations

## Status

**F0 LOW-VOLTAGE PROTOTYPE / MEASUREMENT BUILD.**

This is the physical build companion to `LOCKED_CELL_TOPOLOGY_DC_AC_MIRRORED_GATES.md` and the permanent Virtual Breadboard regressions.

What is already simulated and qualified:

- a stiff center reference between the two outer rails;
- bilateral MOSFET pairs that block both directions OFF and conduct both directions ON;
- three logical Mirror stations;
- finite RC storage and release relative to CENTER;
- the full station shape in which upper and lower mirrored legs meet at a local midpoint and only imbalance current is sent into the CENTER spine.

What is **not** established by this build:

- a spatial `Bx/By/Bz` magnetic field model;
- persistent magnetic memory after drive removal;
- the proposed One-Wave matter, lattice, displacement-pressure, gluonic-tension, or gravity mechanisms.

Those remain later falsification targets. Do not use an LED staying on, a charged capacitor, or an energized coil as proof of magnetic memory or matter hold.

---

## 1. Count boundary — do not drift

```text
3 logical Mirror gates:       G+        G0        G-
2 traversals per Mirror:      ↔         ↔         ↔
6 logical gate traversals total

Each physical Mirror station has:
  upper bilateral electrical leg
  lower bilateral electrical leg

Each bilateral leg uses:
  2 back-to-back N-MOSFETs

Therefore the explicit F0 bench uses:
  3 stations × 2 legs × 2 MOSFETs = 12 MOSFET devices

12 devices DOES NOT mean 12 logical gates.
The logical primitive remains THREE bidirectional Mirror gates.
```

## 2. Rail definition

Use one regulated 5 V low-voltage supply for F0.

```text
absolute bench voltage:       One-Wave relative-to-CENTER name:
NET_P = +5.0 V                +2.5 V
NET_G = +2.5 V                0 / CENTER
NET_N =  0.0 V                -2.5 V
```

`NET_G` is generated by a real source/sink midpoint device such as the TLE2426 already documented in `03_ELECTRICAL_CORE/TLE2426_VIRTUAL_GROUND.md`.

A two-resistor divider is **not** the CENTER return rail.

Preferred physical order on the breadboard:

```text
controller / rail origin                                               far end

NET_P  +5 V  ==============================================================

              [ G+ ]             [ G0 ]             [ G- ]

NET_G  2.5 V  ==============================================================
              |                  |                  |
            10 ohm             10 ohm             10 ohm
              |                  |                  |
          local tap +        local tap 0        local tap -

NET_N   0 V  ==============================================================
```

The CENTER spine is a star reference. Do not daisy-chain `G+ -> G0 -> G-` through a thin return wire.

## 3. One complete Mirror station

Build **G0 first**. G+ and G- are copies after G0 passes.

```text
                       UPPER LEG
NET_P +5 V
   |
 RU = 1 k
   |
   D   Q-U1   S-----S   Q-U2   D
   +----|<------------- >|------+
             ^     ^
             |     |
          common source U
             |
        floating gate drive U
             |
             +------------------ tied gates Q-U1/Q-U2

                                  STATION TAP S0
                                        o
                                        |
                                   RG = 10 ohm
                                        |
NET_G 2.5 V ============================+================ CENTER SPINE
                                        |
                                  STATION TAP S0
                                        o
             +--------------------------+
             |
        floating gate drive L
             |
          common source L
             |     |
             v     v
   D   Q-L1   S-----S   Q-L2   D
   +----|<------------- >|------+
   |
 RL = 1 k
   |
NET_N 0 V
                       LOWER LEG
```

The ASCII diode arrows above are only a reminder that the two MOSFET body diodes must oppose. Verify the actual transistor pinout and body-diode orientation from the datasheet for the exact parts in your hand.

### Electrical meaning

When both mirrored legs are ON and `RU = RL`, the station tap naturally sits near CENTER. Current flows mainly:

```text
NET_P -> RU -> upper bilateral pair -> station tap -> lower bilateral pair -> RL -> NET_N
```

The 10-ohm `RG` path should carry very little current in a balanced hold. It exists so an imbalance becomes a measurable CENTER receipt instead of an invisible assumption.

With a deliberate mismatch, for example changing `RU` from 1 k to about 680 ohm, the local tap leans. `RG` then carries the mismatch current while the midpoint host must keep `NET_G` stiff.

## 4. Bilateral MOSFET block

For each upper or lower leg:

```text
terminal A ---- D  Q1  S----S  Q2  D ---- terminal B
                         |
                    COMMON SOURCE
                         |
                  100 k gate-source
                         |
             tied gates Q1 + Q2
                         |
                      220 ohm
                         |
                    ON switch
                         |
                + floating gate cell

floating gate-cell minus ---------------- COMMON SOURCE
```

The two sources are tied together. The two gates are tied together. Their body diodes then oppose, preventing an OFF path from simply becoming one ordinary diode drop.

### F0 floating gate drive

For slow manual bench qualification, the simplest independent drive is a small floating battery per bilateral pair:

- one CR2032-class ~3 V cell per bilateral pair;
- cell negative to that pair's common-source node;
- cell positive through a manual SPST switch and 220-ohm resistor to the tied gates;
- 100 k from tied gates to common source so OFF is defined when the switch opens.

This is a **test fixture**, not the final controller. It keeps `VGS` referenced to the floating common-source node instead of incorrectly referencing every gate to NET_N.

At F0 currents of only a few milliamps, a 2N7000-class device is acceptable only if the actual device passes the G0 ON/OFF test at the available gate voltage. Do not assume threshold voltage means fully ON. If it does not pass, use a genuine low-threshold logic-level N-MOSFET on a breakout or increase the isolated gate-drive voltage while remaining inside that MOSFET's `VGS(max)`.

## 6. RC hold branch at each station

Add only after the pure G0 electrical station passes.

```text
station tap ---- RRC 1 k ---- HOLD0
                              |
                           C = 10 uF
                              |
NET_G ========================+
                              |
                         RBLEED = 100 k
                              |
NET_G ========================+
```

Nominal fast time constant:

```text
tau ~= RRC × C = 1,000 × 10 uF = 10 ms
```

The 100 k bleed prevents the node from being permanently floating and gives a much slower discharge path when the active feed is isolated.

Interpretation boundary:

```text
capacitor voltage persisting after a command = RC storage
NOT magnetic memory
NOT matter hold
```

## 7. Breadboard placement

Use three long horizontal reference lines and three vertical station zones.

```text
LEFT / HOME                                                        RIGHT

[5 V input]
    |
[TLE2426]---- G-HOME ========================================== G-FAR
    |                 |                 |                 |
NET_P =============== G+ ============== G0 ============== G- =====
                      |                 |                 |
                    station           station           station
                     tap +             tap 0             tap -
                      |                 |                 |
                    10 ohm            10 ohm            10 ohm
                      |                 |                 |
NET_G =============================================================

NET_N =============================================================
```

Keep these layout rules:

1. TLE2426 and its input bypass capacitors live at the **home/controller end**.
2. Run NET_G as one thick spine.
3. Each station's 10-ohm receipt resistor goes directly from its local tap to that spine.
4. Do not route one station's center receipt through another station.
5. Keep the six floating gate batteries/drives electrically isolated from NET_N except through their own common-source reference.
6. Put test points before adding LEDs. LEDs are optional indicators and must have their own resistors; they are not measurement instruments.

## 8. Power-up sequence — do not skip ahead

### P0 — power OFF continuity

With no supply connected:

- verify no direct short NET_P-to-NET_N;
- verify NET_G is not accidentally tied to NET_N;
- verify each MOSFET pair is source-to-source;
- verify 100 k gate-source pulls are installed;
- verify every electrolytic polarity.

### P1 — rail host only

Connect 5 V with a 10–20 mA current limit. No Mirror station connected yet.

Measure:

```text
V(P-N)      about 5.0 V
V(P-G)      about 2.5 V
V(G-N)      about 2.5 V
G-far-home  as close to 0 V as the wiring permits
```

If G is not centered or oscillates, stop. Do not attach the gates.

### P2 — midpoint stiffness

Temporarily place 2.2 k from P to G, then remove it. Place 2.2 k from G to N, then remove it. This asks the midpoint host to source then sink roughly a milliamp. G must remain near the midpoint in both directions.

### P3 — passive station, MOSFETs bypassed

Before adding MOSFET uncertainty, build one 1 k / station-tap / 1 k divider from P to N and connect its tap to G through 10 ohm.

Expected:

```text
station tap ~ G
I through upper arm ~ I through lower arm
voltage across 10 ohm ~ near 0
```

### P4 — deliberate lean

Replace the upper 1 k temporarily with about 680 ohm. A convenient temporary approximation is 1 k in parallel with 2.2 k (~688 ohm).

Expected:

- station tap shifts slightly positive relative to G because the 10-ohm receipt shunt clamps it;
- a measurable millivolt-scale voltage appears across the 10-ohm resistor;
- `I_G = V(10-ohm)/10 ohm`;
- NET_G itself remains close to the supply midpoint.

### P5 — G0 upper bilateral pair

Restore both 1 k arm resistors. Insert only the upper back-to-back MOSFET pair.

OFF:

- no ordinary ~0.7 V one-diode clamp should appear;
- branch current should be leakage-scale.

ON:

- current should return to the low-milliamp range;
- measure actual `VDS` across the pair and calculate effective ON resistance.

### P6 — G0 lower bilateral pair

Add the lower pair. Turn both upper/lower pairs ON. The complete G0 station should now pass P-to-N current through both mirrored legs while the 10-ohm CENTER receipt stays near zero in balance.

### P7 — G0 RC hold

Add the 1 k / 10 uF / 100 k branch. Apply a lean for at least 3 tau (~30 ms), return to balance, and watch HOLD0 decay toward G. Record the time to 63%, 37%, and near-zero differential.

### P8 — copy to G+ and G-

Only after G0 works, duplicate it twice without changing component values. Re-run balance and lean receipts after each added station.

### P9 — slow bidirectional cycling

Begin with manual switching, not high frequency. Alternate the lean polarity and confirm:

```text
positive lean -> positive station receipt
balanced      -> receipt returns toward zero
negative lean -> negative station receipt
```

Then increase frequency slowly while watching G-home, G-far, station tap, and HOLD node. Any G oscillation is a failed reference, not useful AC.

### P10 — magnetic sense only

Add a small sense coil or Hall probe around/near G0. Do **not** close reinjection feedback yet.

The first magnetic question is only:

```text
Does measured field/flux follow current and polarity reproducibly?
```

The memory question comes later:

```text
After electrical drive is removed, is there a repeatable retained magnetic state
that survives controls and cannot be explained by RC charge or instrument offset?
```

## 9. Scope and meter warning

Do not automatically clip a mains-earth-referenced oscilloscope ground lead onto NET_G. Depending on the supply and scope, that can force the synthesized midpoint to earth and destroy the measurement you are trying to make.

Use a differential probe, isolated/battery instrument, or measure each point with a verified safe common reference. Know whether the bench supply negative and scope ground are earth bonded before connecting them.

## 10. Pass/fail numbers for F0

Use these as starting engineering limits, not universal laws:

```text
Supply:                         5 V nominal
Initial current limit:          <= 20 mA
CENTER error unloaded:          < 10 mV from half-supply
CENTER error under F0 lean:     < 25 mV from half-supply
Balanced 10-ohm receipt:        aim < 5 mV (= <0.5 mA imbalance)
MOSFET OFF branch:              no diode-clamped conduction; leakage only
MOSFET ON branch:               stable low-mA conduction, no heating
RC tau target:                  about 10 ms for 1 k / 10 uF
Temperature:                    no part noticeably heating at F0 currents
Magnetic memory claim:          FAIL/UNPROVEN until drive-off retention passes controls
```

The simulator's permanent qualification is intentionally tighter than the physical breadboard limits because the numerical model has no jumper/contact/instrument error.

## 11. Magnetic hold experiment after electrical F0 passes

A safe first geometry using an available ferrite ring:

```text
station-current conductor = one primary pass through ferrite aperture
sense winding             = 20–50 turns fine insulated wire on the ferrite
sense output              = scope/meter only; no reinjection
```

Run four receipts:

1. positive current pulse;
2. negative current pulse;
3. same electrical pulse with the ferrite removed or replaced by a nonmagnetic geometry control;
4. drive removed and the same delayed measurement window used every time.

Only a reproducible drive-off retained signal that reverses with prior polarity and beats the controls earns the name **magnetic hold candidate**.

Do not confuse inductive decay immediately after opening the circuit with remanent memory. Reverse recovery, RC discharge, and scope offset must be separated first.

## 12. What comes after F0

Do not add active magnetic reinjection, three-winding actuator power, motor load, memristive elements, or XYZ field claims until F0 receipts show that:

```text
CENTER stays stiff
three station taps balance
three stations lean predictably
all bilateral legs really block both directions OFF
all bilateral legs conduct in both intended directions ON
RC timing matches measured values
magnetic sensing is reproducible
```

Then the next prototype can replace the six temporary floating gate batteries with a coordinated isolated gate-driver/controller layer and test three-winding magnetic actuation without changing the three logical Mirror-gate count.
