# C2 — Walk Cadence / Pose Beat Fitter

**Status: RUNTIME PASS**

C2 converts project FPS into even pose holds for a selected consecutive pose run. Default production preset is 24 FPS at 6 pose beats/sec = 4-frame holds. The final pose can receive an extra turnaround multiplier.

Chromium verification passed with 7 poses: holds became 4,4,4,4,4,4,12 and pose artwork remained unchanged.
