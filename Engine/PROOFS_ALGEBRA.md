## Theorem 1 — two routes, one number, two receipts

**Claim.** For integers N, m,

$$
2N + 2m = 2(N+m).
$$

**Proof.** Right side: \(2N+2m\). Left side is the same polynomial. Equality of values does not identify routes: `R_after(N,2m)=2N+2m` and `R_before(N,m)=2(N+m)` remain distinct receipts.

## Theorem 2 — shared nest boundary

$$
2N+1 = 2(N+1)-1.
$$

**Proof.** Right: \(2N+2-1=2N+1\). So the +1 wrapper of nest N is the −1 wrapper of nest N+1. Adjacent packets share a station.

## Theorem 3 — inverse recovers N

If \(X=2N+m\) then \(N=(X-m)/2\).
If \(X=2(N+m)\) then \(N=X/2-m\).
If wrapper \(s\in\{-1,+1\}\) is recorded and stripped first, the same inverses recover N exactly on the integers.

**Proof.** Substitute. Division without stripping s or m is not the inverse (anti-drift).

## Theorem 4 — wrapper has opposite parity

X even ⇒ X±1 odd. X odd ⇒ X±1 even. Immediate from parity of 1.

## Theorem 5 — alphabet inversion is an involution

\(N_{\mathrm{inv}}=27-N\) on \(\{1,\ldots,26\}\). Then \(N_{\mathrm{inv}}\in\{1,\ldots,26\}\) and \((N_{\mathrm{inv}})_{\mathrm{inv}}=N\).

## Theorem 6 — fifths rail, no semitone inside the engine

On \(\mathbb{Z}/12\mathbb{Z}\):

$$
+7 \equiv -5, \qquad -7 \equiv +5.
$$

**Proof.** \(7+5=12\equiv 0\). Mirror through six: \((6+k)+(6-k)=12\equiv 0\). Station 12 \(\equiv 0\) on the next cycle. Station 13 \(\equiv 1\) on the next cycle.

This is modular arithmetic on a declared 12-station rail. It does **not** prove 12-TET pitch physics. Gray keeps semitones on the SM plate.

## Theorem 7 — χ is linear in wakes

By definition

$$
\chi(x)=\sum_w a_w W(|x-x_w|;\sigma_w).
$$

Hence

$$
\chi_{\mathrm{parent+locals}}-\chi_{\mathrm{locals}}=a_{\mathrm{parent}}W_{\mathrm{parent}}.
$$

If \(a_{\mathrm{parent}}>0\) and \(W>0\) off a null set, removing the parent **must** change χ. That is the top-down curvature claim as a **sum**, not as cosmology.
