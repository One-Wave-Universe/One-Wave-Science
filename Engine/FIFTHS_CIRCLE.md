# Fifths circle

```
        +5
     +4    +3
  +2          +1
-5      0       +5   wait: ring is 0 with ±1..±5 and 6 opposite
        6
```

Use only: `0`, `-5..-1`, `+1..+5`, `6`.

Gray residue `+7` means the same walk as `-5` the other way. Do not draw a 7.

Major `-5(0)+4`. Minor `-5(0)+3`. Aug `-5(0)+5` or `-4(0)+4`.
