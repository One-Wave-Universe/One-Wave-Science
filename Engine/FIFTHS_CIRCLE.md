# Fifths circle (One-Wave labels)

Do not number this clock 0,1,2,…,11 like a chromatic hour ring.

**Spoken / used names:**

- `0` tonic / local zero (12 of the last cycle is this 0)
- `-1 -2 -3 -4 -5` back
- `+1 +2 +3 +4 +5` forward
- `6` straight across

There is no hour named 7, 8, 9, 10, or 11 on this circle.

`+7` in older notes is **not a slot**. It is the Gray residue name for the same step as `-5` the other way. Same for `-7` = `+5` the other way. If you say seven you already left this circle.

Mirror: from any tonic at 0, across is 6. From 6, across is 0.

Chords stay in these names:

- Major `-5 (0) +4`
- Minor `-5 (0) +3`
- Aug balanced `-5 (0) +5` or `-4 (0) +4`

Chromatic `Aut(Z/12)` in `CHROMATIC_CIRCLE.md` is Gray machinery under the hood. Do not put 7–11 on the fifths drawing.
