# Rail — six, twelve, thirteen

Brick: YELLOW lock of addressing language. Not a particle. Not T6.

## Cycle and double

Working Field cycle is **six** (BEGIN MOVE HOLD BUILD BREAK LOOP).
Address rail is **twelve** (hop 1..12).
ORIGINAL: TOP = 2N. Six doubled is twelve.

## Mirror

Mirror lives at **six** on the twelve-station rail.
AXIS = 0. Opposite = 6. +6 and −6 are the same station.
If you stand on six, six is the new AXIS and its TOP is twelve.

## Wrap

12 is the next cycle's 0.
13 is +1 on the next cycle.
11 is −1 on the next cycle.

Packet `6|12|13`:
- 6 this flip
- 12 next AXIS / next 0
- 13 next AXIS upper wrapper

Packet `6|12|11` is the other mandatory wrapper.

13 is not a 13th class.

## Fifth = wrap of the ±5 span

+7 ≡ −5 (mod 12). −7 ≡ +5 (mod 12).
CLOCKWISE = 3:2 child = land on the +7 ≡ −5 station.
COUNTERCLOCKWISE = 2:3 parent = land on the −7 ≡ +5 station.
±5 is one short of the six-wall. ±7 is one past it. Same landings through the wrap.

No semitone primitive. Gray may print 12-TET. Engine does not.

## Hop

N in 1..12. TOP = 2N. Wrappers 2N±1. No packet, no dive, no rise.
Octave k is speed 2^k on a folded envelope, then N = clip(6+k, 1, 12).
