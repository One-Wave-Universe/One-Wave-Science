# Rail chords around local 0

Tonic sits at 0. 12 is the next 0. Mirror through 6 o'clock is +6.

| Shape | Name here | Pitch classes |
|---|---|---|
| -5 (0) 4+ | Major | {0,4,7} |
| -5 (0) 3+ | Minor | {0,3,7} |
| -5 (0) 5+ | Augmented, no lean | {0,5,7} |
| -4 (0) 4+ | Augmented, no lean | {0,4,8} |

Major/Minor lean is the unequal + side. Augmented ±4 or ±5 is balanced.
+7 one way is still -5 the other, so the shared -5 leg is the fifth-rail into 0.
