# Two opposing rotations

Close is **on 6**, not before it. The seat right before 6 is the last lean `±1`.

Forward `+5` from 0:

`0, +5, -2, +3, -4, +1, **6**, -1, +4, -3, +2, -5, 0`

Back `-5` from 0:

`0, -5, +2, -3, +4, -1, **6**, +1, -4, +3, -2, +5, 0`

On 6 they meet. The step before is `+1` vs `-1`. After 6 they swap: forward is suddenly on `-1`, back on `+1`. That is the opposite-direction close.

Home is 0 at step 12. Across-close is 6 at step 6.
