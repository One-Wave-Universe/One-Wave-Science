# Two opposing rotations

`6` is the **halfway mirror**. Closure **begins** there. It **finishes** at `0`.

- Steps 0–6: open / go out. Last lean before the mirror is `±1`.
- On 6: both walks occupy the same seat. Signs swap after this point.
- Steps 6–12: close / come home. Finish at `0`.

Forward `+5`:
`0, +5, -2, +3, -4, +1, **6**, -1, +4, -3, +2, -5, **0**`

Back `-5`:
`0, -5, +2, -3, +4, -1, **6**, +1, -4, +3, -2, +5, **0**`

Halfway = begin-close. Home = closed.
