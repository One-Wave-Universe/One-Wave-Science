# Two opposing rotations

Not one fifths arrow. Two mirrored walks that close the other way.

- Forward rotation: repeat `+5` from `0`.
- Back rotation: repeat `-5` from `0`.
- They meet at `6` after six steps each.
- They both return to `0` after twelve steps.

6 is the close-across. 0 is the close-home. Opposite direction, same meeting.

Spoken seats only: `0`, `±1…±5`, `6`.
