Why a general solver, not a formula

Poincaré (1889) proved the three-body problem has no general closed-form solution in elementary functions — motion for generic starting conditions is chaotic. This page numerically integrates Newton's equations of motion directly (the way the problem is actually "solved" in practice) and verifies correctness live via energy / momentum / angular-momentum conservation.
