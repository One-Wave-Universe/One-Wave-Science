# G-762 — Four balanced interactions

At a hold, nothing wins.

\[
\overline E_4 = \langle E_K + E_E + E_M + E_T + E_\times \rangle
\]

\[
\nabla_q \overline E_4(q_0)=0,\qquad
P_{\rm ext}(\xi=0)=P_K+P_E+P_M+P_T+P_\times=0.
\]

Each \(P_a=dE_a/d\xi\) is signed. Assist or resist is computed, not declared by name.

## Who sits on whom

| | Compress (In) | Express (Out) | Meet (Across) | Flip (Over) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Knot | tightens circulation | loosens | phase-meets ring | winding jump |
| Shell | radius in | radius out | two-face pressure | shell inversion |
| Mirror | loads the seam | unloads the seam | both faces on one Ground | basin change |
| Weave | binds skin to knot | lets skin slip | shared tension | re-knit after flip |
| Cross | mix of the above | mix | mix | mix |

Balance means those rows sum to zero *at rest*. Mass Effect is the cost of moving the whole table. Gate is the cost of the Over column going through.

## What unbalance is

- One row dominates and the hold dies (no recurrence).
- Crosses zeroed: four separate SM-like forces. Forbidden as a derivation.
- Weave wins, Mirror dipole dies: G-760. No second basin.
- Mirror wins, weave dies: a lopsided ring with no skin. Not a proton-like hold.

The Micro fight is to find coefficients where Knot, Shell, Mirror, Weave all sit nonzero at one \(q_0\) *and* a second \(q_\pi\) with the same \(\overline E_4\).

## Not

Not four gauge groups. Not four particles. Not four windings. Three windings are the nerve. Four interactions are the hold.
