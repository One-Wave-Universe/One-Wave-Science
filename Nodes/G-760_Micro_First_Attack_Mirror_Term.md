# G-760 — Attack the Mirror term first

G-735 said Micro until the knot holds. C-322 said two basins then a path. The Hessian at finite dipole looked like two minima. Descent says otherwise.

## Result

`E_M = alpha sin^2 theta` is singular at vanishing dipole (G-757 receipt).

`E_M = alpha S^2` is smooth and **one valley**. Both seeds relax to `S=0`, `E=0`. No barrier. NEB is a flat line.

`E_M = alpha S^2 + mu (C^2+S^2-m^2)^2` should pin two wells at `C=\pm m`. On the seven-cell with the present weave / roughness (`beta_K`, `beta_T`) the ring flattens anyway. Relaxed states have `C=0`, `S=0`. Barrier ~ 0.

So the first Micro fight is not NEB, not W, not 125 GeV. It is:

> Can a Mirror potential that wants a finite dipole survive the weave that wants a round ring?

If no coefficient set does that, G-757 does not have two basins and C-322 has no gate on this graph.

## Attack order (do not skip)

1. Coefficient scan: `mu` vs `beta_T` vs `beta_K`. Map the region where two relaxed minima keep `|C| > 0` and opposite sign.
2. Only in that region, Hessian at both minima.
3. Only then NEB and saddle Hessian (one negative mode).
4. Only then W and carry (Mass Effect).
5. Proton-knot observables last.

The HTML and the zine wait on step 1.
