# GEM field analogies

Linearized weak-field GR ↔ Maxwell shape. No graviton required. Interaction 4 in `FOUR_INTERACTIONS.md`.

## Pair

| EM | GEM |
|---|---|
| E | E_g (g, fall) |
| B | B_g (frame-drag / mass-current) |
| ρ | ρ_m |
| J | J_m |
| photon-stamp | κ-mode stamp if a detector ever couples |

Gauss for gravity carries a **minus** vs EM: mass attracts (same-sign charge would repel).

\[
\nabla\cdot\mathbf{E}_g = -4\pi G\rho_m
\qquad
\nabla\cdot\mathbf{B}_g = 0
\]
\[
\nabla\times\mathbf{E}_g + \partial_t\mathbf{B}_g = 0
\]
\[
\nabla\times\mathbf{B}_g - \frac{1}{c^2}\partial_t\mathbf{E}_g = -\frac{4\pi G}{c^2}\mathbf{J}_m
\]

Potentials: \(\mathbf{E}_g=-\nabla\phi_g-\partial_t\mathbf{A}_g\), \(\mathbf{B}_g=\nabla\times\mathbf{A}_g\).
Some books put 1/2 on \(\mathbf{A}_g\) / \(\mathbf{B}_g\) because the tensor is spin-2 in the linear theory. Same physics, different packing.

Lorentz analog: \(\mathbf{a} \sim \mathbf{E}_g + \mathbf{v}\times\mathbf{B}_g\).

## Limits

Weak field, slow compared to c, almost-flat chart. Lense–Thirring / Gravity Probe B is B_g of a spinning Earth — real, tiny.
Not a lift on a 12 V star. The cell walk is the *shape* of Ampere for charge-current. GEM says mass-current has that shape with G/c^{2} stiffness.

One-wave: E_g is −∇κ language. B_g is rotating leftover order of the κ-mode. Faraday analog = changing B_g shifts the path cost. No fifth interaction. No bullet.
