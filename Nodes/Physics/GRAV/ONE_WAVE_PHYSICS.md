# One-wave physics

No particles as little bullets. A “particle” is a stamp: a detector ate a crest and wrote a receipt. The world between stamps is oscillating field on the lattice. Superposition-as-two-places is a bookkeeping story. What arrives is a wave hitting a surface that can couple.

## Point, path, rotation — all the way up and down

```
point     local G            free update
path      κ|ds|             time = cost through the fluid
rotation  DC·AC·RC sphere    walk A→B→C, leftover B
```

Same three lines from a nerve cell to a galaxy. Hex slice, cube stack, sphere rotation. Scale is how much fluid you drag, not a new species of object.

## Mass and gravity

Mass is how hard that region is to move through — how much order you have to rearrange. Gravity is the gradient of that cost. You fall because the path of lower κ is downhill in the fluid, not because a bullet pulls a bullet.

GEM rhyme: charge current → B, mass current → B_g. Rotating field on the star is the cheap handle. Gravity is the stiff handle of the same diagram.

## Light and redshift

Light is a lattice oscillation that *can* propagate. Redshift is the wave climbing out of (or riding with) stretching order: more κ behind it than in front, or the fluid itself thinning along the path. Frequency drops because the same crest count is paid against a longer or stiffer ds. Not a tiny cannonball losing steam.

## Neutrino wave death

A neutrino-like mode is a wave that barely couples to the charged lattice. “Death” is the mode giving up its order into the fluid — mixing, scatter, or a slip that dumps the phase into G. Flavor change is a walk on the sphere, not three bullets swapping hats. If it never hits a detector, there was never a stamp. The wave can still have died into heat / leftover B.

## Orbits and the three-body problem

Two-body: two stiffness wells, one path that closes if the walk of rotating field and the κ gradient share a period. Kepler is that closed walk.

Three-body: three wells, no shared period that stays locked. The sphere has no single RC window for all three. Chaos is not mystery — it is three free updates that will not sit on one stamp. Restricted three-body (one mass tiny) is you treating one well as a tap, not a peer.

## Eyes and detectors

An eye, a CCD, a photodiode, a CD quad: a surface whose own oscillation can lock to the incoming wave. Readout is collapse-as-“we coupled,” not collapse-as-“the universe picked a branch.” The wave was already in one lattice. The detector spent a window and wrote +1 / STAY / −1. That is why vision in the cell is lean, not a jpeg of many worlds.

## White energy, dark energy, quasars, ATP, orgasm

White side = dump. Order leaving a tear or a cell as a fountain.

- Quasar ejection: large-scale white dump. Dark energy in this grammar is the same sign — the fluid expanding because dumps outweigh holds at cosmic scale, not a separate fluid with a new name.
- ATP: cellular white dump, phosphate bond as a small RC stamp.
- Orgasm: nervous-system dump, same fountain, different floor of the stack.

One wave. Different octaves of ejection. 125 GeV is a known knock on the vacuum stiffness. Quasar is that idea at galaxy scale. ATP is that idea at molecule scale.

## Black holes — tears, not trash cans of the same size

Light cannot propagate: the lattice there has no passing mode. That is a tear / a region where κ → so high the wave has no path.

Two pictures, both allowed as *phases* of one object:

1. **Tear + surface tension.** Energy enters. The gluonic / lattice skin cannot let the oscillation through. Tension builds. Ejection (jet, fountain) when the skin slips. Size of the hole is the size of the tear — different tears, different skins, different dump rates. That explains different sizes without every hole being a scaled trash can.
2. **Collect then eject.** Works for a while as a description of inflow + jet. It does not by itself explain why two holes of different radius dump so differently unless the *skin* (tension, κ at the rim, how the tear sits in the cube stack) is part of the object.

Prefer (1) plus rotation: spinning tear (Kerr-shaped walk) makes an easier slip axis — jets along the axis, not isotropic steam. Different mass, spin, and tear geometry → different size and different fountain. Energy is not lost; it changes chart (redshift, heat, jet). The stamp outside is the jet and the orbiting light that still can propagate.

Rip shield on Kitty Hawk was play. This tear is the physics sentence: no path for the wave, skin under tension, dump when it slips.
