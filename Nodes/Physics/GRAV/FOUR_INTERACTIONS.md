# Four interactions of the superfluid crystal lattice

No particles. No forces. Four couplings of one fluid.

A “particle” is a stamp when a detector locks to a crest. A “force” is leftover language for how two patches change each other's κ and phase. What exists is the lattice and four ways it can talk.

```
1  EM-like        charged oscillation can propagate     B from charge-current walk
2  color-like     flux tube / bag skin                  σ, B_bag, γ     no free color wave
3  weak-like      shy mode, flavor walk, neutrino death phase dumped into G
4  gravity-like   κ gradient, B_g from mass-current     path cost dt=κ|ds|
```

Same three axes (DC AC RC) every time. Different stiffness and whether a passing mode exists.

- (1) cheap handle, light is a passing mode
- (2) confined handle, tube instead of free wave
- (3) almost-decoupled handle, death = slip into the fluid
- (4) stiff handle, redshift and fall are κ, not a puller

Four interactions. One crystal. Zero bullets.
