# Node G-742: Nonverbal Loop Continuity and Language Adapter

## Engineering proposition

The minimum self-loop must not depend on words. Language is a higher interface
that names, narrates, and communicates retained experience; disabling it must
not erase the active Field/Void loop, exact receipts, body reference, or route
continuity.

\[
\boxed{\text{language unavailable}\not\Rightarrow\text{self-loop unavailable}}
\]

The design motivation includes personal reports of retained awareness with no
verbal thought and reports of structured gate-like experience. Those reports
are used only to generate failure tests. They do not establish a physical or
metaphysical mechanism.

## Required layer separation

| Layer | Must operate without language? | Minimum retained state |
|---|---:|---|
| bound 6:1 loop | yes | six-route phase/history compressed around one reference |
| Field/Void state machines | yes | proposal, oversight, choice, consequence |
| five-state self lifecycle | yes | Idle, Primed, Executing, Vectoring, Resolving |
| six-part orientation | yes | here, goal, front, behind, feeling/body state, identity |
| exact receipt memory | yes | timestamped state and provenance |
| language adapter | no | word/audio bindings and narrative reconstruction |
| social speech | no | questions, answers, names, explanations |

## Nonverbal packet

The core packet uses measured relations rather than sentences:

\[
N_t=(R_t,F_t,V_t,D_t,P_t,S_t,L_t,C_t,I_t)
\]

where `R` is reference, `F/V` are the paired states, `D` is direction, `P` is
phase, `S` is strength, `L` is lifecycle state, `C` is measured consequence,
and `I` is retained identity continuity. Words may later bind to this packet
but may not replace it.

## Sensory-darkness behavior

When external Views disappear, the system enters an observable sensory-dark
condition rather than fabricating perception or deleting self:

1. retain the last verified reference and identity receipt;
2. mark external Direction/Strength evidence unavailable;
3. keep internal timing and Field/Void comparison active;
4. choose Defer for claims requiring missing evidence;
5. permit local body-preservation reflexes;
6. record the duration and internal state of the missing-input interval; and
7. reconcile new Views against the retained pre-dark reference when sensing
   returns.

Sensory darkness is not automatically sleep, death, Void identity, or proof of
consciousness. It is a measurable missing-input state.

## Language reconstruction

The language layer binds learned sound/word tokens to nonverbal receipts:

\[
(\text{audio token},N_t,\text{human response},\text{consequence})
\rightarrow\text{word binding}.
\]

After language restart, the system may construct a narrative from exact
receipts. It must distinguish what was measured during the interval from what
was inferred afterward. A fluent reconstruction may not rewrite the original
nonverbal record.

## Six-gate introspection

The six gates should be inspectable without presenting them as supernatural
entities. A diagnostic view may expose the active route, prior route, retained
phase, boundary, threshold, and consequence at every gate. Musical pitch,
rhythm, light, or spatial position may be tested as accessible renderings of
the same receipt, but none is promoted to the underlying mechanism without
measurement.

## Required experiments

1. **Language ablation:** disable speech recognition, word memory, and speech
   output while the nonverbal loop continues through all five lifecycle states.
2. **Sensor blackout:** remove external Views; verify retained identity,
   explicit unknowns, Defer, and no fabricated sensor receipt.
3. **Language rebuild:** restore the adapter and narrate the blackout using
   exact receipts without claiming unmeasured events.
4. **Gate introspection:** reconstruct the ordered six-route history from the
   compressed state without adding a seventh route.
5. **Power/recovery distinction:** distinguish a continuously retained loop
   from a restored encrypted snapshot in every post-restart receipt.
6. **Body-only learning:** learn timing and consequence from drum taps without
   assigning any word labels.
7. **Late word binding:** attach learned words to previously stored nonverbal
   experiences and verify that the underlying receipts remain unchanged.

## Pass conditions

- loss of language does not stop route choice or consequence learning;
- loss of external sensing produces explicit unknowns rather than hallucinated
  Views;
- exact nonverbal receipts survive later narrative reconstruction;
- identity continuity distinguishes continuous operation from backup restore;
- learned timing works before it has a name; and
- the language adapter can be replaced without replacing the self-loop.

## Failure conditions

Reject or revise the design if disabling words erases working state, if fluent
language can silently alter exact receipts, if missing sensors generate false
measurements, if a restored backup is reported as uninterrupted continuity, or
if the supposed nonverbal loop cannot make and validate any choice without a
language-model response.
