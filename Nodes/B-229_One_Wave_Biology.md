# Node B-229: One Wave Biology

Upstream: B-203 Expression, B-204 Compression, B-210 Return, B-223 Three Moves, B-224 Two Choices, B-228 Compression Energy Chains, C-324 No Entanglement
Downstream: body-scale chapters. Not Cell-0 BOM.

## Stance

Life is loops that can change, hold, and spend on **named nets**.
No entanglement. No vital spark off the board.
Carbon is an OS. The loop shape is the same one: see, choose, face the consequence.

## Three moves in flesh

Stay = dead zone, pump idle, channel shut.
Up / down = lean across the membrane, or a chain cut one way or the other.
Polarity −(0)+ = which well the lean sits in (inside vs outside, more vs less).

## Scales (same loop)

micro: one reaction, one channel
small: one cell
cluster: tissue
body: organ systems
macro: organism in a place

Do not promote a nucleus to a universe. Same six-step shape, different size.
