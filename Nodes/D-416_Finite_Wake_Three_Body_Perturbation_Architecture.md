## Relational rule

For bodies `i, j`:

`Delta_ij = grad(Phi_i) - grad(Phi_j)`

The system state is the closed relational loop of pair edges (Sun-Earth,
Earth-Moon, Moon-Sun for the base test), not three absolute coordinates.
A closure identity that follows algebraically from pair differences is a
bookkeeping tautology, not evidence; only a non-tautological prediction
about evolution, stability, residuals, or a boundary condition counts.

replaces summing independent wells to infinity. A body's wake is

Candidate assimilation quantities: displacement amplitude, gradient
contrast, phase coherence, curvature contrast, scale ratio, or a
hysteretic combination. No cutoff radius may be inserted ad hoc to fit
orbital data.
