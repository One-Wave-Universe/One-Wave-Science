## What is established (chemistry)

Sugars and fats are **short and long carbon chains** the cell can break.
Breaking them is stepwise compression of those chains into smaller pieces plus carriers.
ATP is a **small reusable packet**: ADP + Pi ↔ ATP. The usable kick is the phosphate transfer, not a singularity.
No net to a distant twin. Mitochondrion is a named organelle with named membranes and currents.

## What this node is allowed to say (analogy)

One Wave bookkeeping:

- **Expression** = chain stored, lean not yet spent (sugar / fat as path).
- **Compression** = chain driven into a tight well (enzymes + membrane potential).
- **Release / return** = packet handed off (ATP) so another loop can move.
- **Hold** = the packet stays until a named enzyme spends it.

Macro compression-release (quasar-scale language in older notes) and body climax language are **the same three moves at another scale**: load, squeeze, spend. They are not the same object as ATP.

"Micro black hole" in this node means only: **a local compression well with a fence**. It does not mean an astrophysical hole in the cell. Do not build one. Do not claim Hawking bookkeeping for mitochondria.
