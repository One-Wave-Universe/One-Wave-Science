# G-736 — Standard Model Interpretation Overlay for Micro Simulators

**Status:** YELLOW visualization contract / mechanism mapping open  
**Dependencies:** G-732, G-735, Book 1 Chapters 1–6 and 13–15

## Layer contract

Micro simulators preserve three separable layers:

1. **One-Wave mechanism:** continuous amplitude, phase, vortex cores, Paths,
   enclosing Field, boundaries, and measured interaction receipts.
2. **Gray interpretation:** established Standard Model labels and measured
   quantities placed over the mechanism without being silently redefined.
3. **Combined view:** synchronized labels and mechanism state, with every
   unproved mapping visibly marked as interpretation rather than derivation.

## Proton candidate overlay

The first spherical prototype displays:

- three identity-preserving internal vortex candidates;
- pairwise coupling links;
- internal Point rotation, carried Paths, and enclosing spherical Field phase;
- cutaway, rotation, and zoom;
- optional `uud`, net `+1e`, instantaneous `R/G/B`, and gluon-link labels.

The `R/G/B` tags are QCD bookkeeping labels, not literal visible colors. Their
assignment rotates in the educational overlay to avoid implying that one core
permanently owns one color. The combined `uud` state is labeled color-singlet
only as the Gray interpretation; deriving SU(3) gauge behavior from the
One-Wave mechanism remains open.

## Claim boundary

The current spherical motion is a kinematic visualization driven by the
validated point-vortex control, not a 3D QCD calculation and not yet an evolved
nonlinear One-Wave proton solution. It becomes physical evidence only after the
PDE engine preserves winding, binding, energy, charge mapping, spin, and
measured proton observables under perturbation.
