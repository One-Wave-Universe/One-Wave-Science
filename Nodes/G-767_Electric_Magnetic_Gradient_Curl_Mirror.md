# G-767 — Electric–Magnetic Gradient/Curl Mirror

## Candidate correspondence
One-Wave currently tests the same-medium correspondence

```
E ~ grad(P_c)
B ~ curl(P_c)
```

as a way to distinguish radial/gradient and rotational/curl behavior.

## Boundary
The phrases **"magnetism is electricity mirrored"**, **"rotated 90 degrees"**, and **"reflected across the null"** are interpretive hypotheses until a transformation is derived that maps the candidate electric observable into the magnetic observable while preserving the required Maxwell constraints.

## Derivation target
Find an operator M such that, under declared conditions,

```
B = M[E]
```

and determine whether M is legitimately a geometric rotation, a duality transformation, a phase relation, or none of these.

The derivation must recover the relevant divergence/curl structure and dimensions rather than relying on analogy.

## CELL consequence under test
If magnetic response is physical reorganization of the active lattice, CELL flips and retained state may be modeled as reorientation rather than abstract switching. That interpretation remains unproved.

## Pass gate
PASS only when the transformation, units, conservation law, and Maxwell-limit comparison close consistently.
