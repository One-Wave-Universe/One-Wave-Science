# G-768 — 2D–3D Six/Twelve Wrapper Derivation

## Problem
The repository contains the structural ladder

```
3:1 <-> 1(0)1 <-> 1:6
6:1 <-> 1(0)1 <-> 1:12
12:1 <-> 1(0)1 <-> 1:24
```

and uses 2D hex/triangular geometry plus 3D wrapper concepts. The physical necessity of the 6/12 counts has **not yet been derived**.

## Target
Derive rather than assume the state counts.

For each dimensional wrapper:
1. define the independent axes/degrees of freedom;
2. define mirrored +/- orientation and the null/reference;
3. enumerate unique directed states after symmetry equivalences;
4. show why 2D produces the proposed six routes;
5. show whether 3D necessarily produces twelve routes;
6. determine exactly what the claimed -6...+12 range means: orientation, state index, route count, or another quantity.

## Rejection rule
If the count follows only because six or twelve directions were inserted into the geometry at the start, it is circular and does not pass.

## Cross-domain candidates
Rabbit Hop, six-step/triple-flip, A/B/C hex geometry, PPF, and Circle of Fifths may be used as comparison structures, but none counts as proof of another domain without equation, physical coupling, simulation, or measurement.
