# G-750 — Three-Alpha Hoyle-State Derivation Target

## Purpose

Test whether One-Wave can derive a three-alpha carbon-12 resonance corresponding to the Hoyle state without inserting its measured energy.

## Target

Start from one common interaction/boundary/rotation law for three alpha clusters.

Derive:
- allowed symmetric configurations;
- rotational/vibrational modes;
- a (0^+) excited resonance;
- its energy relative to the triple-alpha threshold;
- its width/lifetime or an approximation;
- the route into the carbon-12 ground-state band.

Then use the derived resonance in the triple-alpha reaction rate.

## Anti-fitting rule

The measured Hoyle-state energy may not be used to tune a private coupling whose purpose is to place the resonance at that energy.

A successful L3 recovery requires parameters fixed independently.

## Strong next prediction

After recovering the known state, predict another measurable carbon-12 excitation, branching ratio, rotational-band property, or rate correction before consulting that target datum.

## Failure condition

If no near-threshold three-alpha (0^+) resonance emerges without post-hoc tuning, the proposed derivation fails.
