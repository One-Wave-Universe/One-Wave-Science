# E-530 — Solar Coronal Boundary Heating

## Purpose

Test whether the One-Wave boundary/shell picture can produce a quantitative coronal energy-transport law.

## Required energy budget

A candidate model must close

[

Every term must have explicit units and an independently defined physical meaning.

## One-Wave hypothesis

Magnetic/rotational reorganization at a boundary may move energy from lower layers into a hot, weakly bound outer region.

The hypothesis is not complete until it predicts:
- where the energy is deposited;
- the magnitude of the heating flux;
- radial temperature behavior;
- coupling to solar-wind acceleration;
- dependence on field topology.

## Bound/unbound relation

A candidate bound-state variable may be used only if it predicts a measurable change in transport/heating.

No free heating function may be inserted simply to reproduce the corona.

## Failure condition

If "boundary tension" cannot close the energy budget or predict deposition location, the idea remains analogy.
