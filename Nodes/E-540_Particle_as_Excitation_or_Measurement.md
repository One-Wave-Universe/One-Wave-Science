# Node E-540: Particle as Excitation or Measurement

Dependencies:
Upstream: A+101 One Field Ground, A-112 Persistent Mode, E-525 Focal Point Measurement Operator, E-531 Terminal Optical Wave Lifecycle
Downstream: every micro-scale chapter, public summaries, audio and video narration

Definition:
One-Wave contains no fundamental particle substance. “Particle” is permitted only as shorthand for one of two things:

1. an excitation: a localized, bounded, recurring wave configuration in the one field;
2. a measurement: a discrete registered interaction produced when a continuous wave couples to a detector, boundary, focal point, or threshold.

Excitation:
A proton, electron, neutron, photon event, or other named object is treated according to its actual One-Wave node as a stable or traveling wave configuration, shell recurrence, knot, vortex, rupture, mode, or measured transfer. The label does not convert the field pattern into a tiny independent object.

Measurement:
A detector returns a bounded event because the detector is bounded, thresholded, and local. Discreteness in the record does not prove a discrete traveler crossed space as a pellet.

Mandatory Narration Rule:
Every audio, video, summary, or AI answer discussing particles must state near the beginning:

“Particles are not fundamental objects in One-Wave. The word refers to wave excitations or to discrete measurement events.”

Forbidden Compression:
Do not say “particles are waves and particles.” Do not describe photons as little packets flying through empty space. Do not rebuild particle ontology from convenient nouns after the chapter has already removed it.
