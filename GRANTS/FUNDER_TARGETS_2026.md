# Funder Targets — 2026 Snapshot

**Last checked:** 2026-09-21

This is a targeting aid, not an eligibility determination. Confirm the current solicitation before submission.

## 1. NSF America's Seed Fund — SBIR/STTR

### Why it may fit
This is the clearest current path if the project is organized as a U.S. small business and the near-term proposal is framed as a deep-tech product/instrumentation R&D program rather than a broad theory claim.

Current 2026 NSF Seed Fund materials list:
- NSF 26-510 — SBIR/STTR deep technologies;
- NSF 26-511 — pilot emphasis on scientific instrumentation;
- full-proposal deadlines including November 4, 2026 and March 4, 2027;
- a required Project Pitch before a Phase I full proposal.

Current public program pages state that eligible companies must be U.S.-based small businesses and describe additional ownership/PI employment requirements.

### Best repo framing
Lead with:
- Wave Reader V1;
- reproducible experimental platform;
- Virtual Breadboard/BASIS;
- calibrated low-voltage prototype testing;
- commercializable scientific instrumentation/software.

Do not lead with a claim that the entire One-Wave theory is proven.

### Sources
- https://seedfund.nsf.gov/
- https://seedfund.nsf.gov/solicitations/
- https://seedfund.nsf.gov/apply/get-started/
- https://seedfund.nsf.gov/apply/full-proposal/

## 2. NSF EAGER — academic/nonprofit research route

### Why it may fit
EAGER is designed for exploratory, early-stage, potentially transformative research ideas and high-risk/high-reward approaches.

It requires prior engagement with the relevant NSF program; current NSF guidance calls for a Concept Outline before an EAGER proposal.

### Important constraint
EAGER is not a generic startup grant and is not intended simply to collect preliminary data. This route is strongest if an eligible academic/nonprofit PI and an appropriate NSF program officer see the narrow experimental question as suitable.

### Best repo framing
One narrow falsifiable mechanism:
- continuous-medium prediction;
- state-recurrence hardware mechanism;
- unusual instrumentation method.

### Sources
- https://www.nsf.gov/eng/eng-proposal-guidance
- NSF PAPPG EAGER guidance.

## 3. DOE Office of Science — annual/open program route

### Current state
DOE Office of Science lists an FY2026 continuation solicitation for financial assistance with a current close date of September 30, 2026.

DOE states that Office of Science financial-assistance applications must respond to an open FOA and are submitted through Grants.gov. Eligibility varies by FOA.

### Fit
Potentially relevant only after identifying a specific Office of Science program area and institutional eligibility. This is not a good place to send a broad unsolicited theory package.

Potential proposal-facing strengths:
- instrumentation;
- numerical methods;
- scientific computing;
- reproducibility;
- bounded basic-research mechanism tests.

### Sources
- https://science.osti.gov/grants/FOAs/Open
- https://science.osti.gov/grants/FOAs
- https://science.osti.gov/grants/Applicant-and-Awardee-Resources/Grants-Process

## 4. DOE Genesis Mission / AI-for-science pathways

DOE's 2026 funding pages list "The Genesis Mission: Transforming Science and Energy with AI" (DE-FOA-0003612). The currently visible late-2026 deadline applies to Phase II applications resulting from FY26 Phase I awards, so it should not be treated as an open new-entry Phase I opportunity now.

The repo should nevertheless retain an AI-for-science package because future rounds may align with:
- autonomous experiment planning;
- reproducible simulation;
- instrument/data pipelines;
- local AI scientific workers.

## Recommended targeting order

### Near term
1. Determine legal/applicant eligibility.
2. If small-business eligible, prepare an NSF Seed Fund Project Pitch around the experimental platform/instrumentation.
3. In parallel, identify an academic or nonprofit collaborator for an NSF research route.
4. Track DOE annual and topical FOAs rather than forcing a mismatch.

## Targeting rule

Choose the funder based on the **funded unit**:

- instrumentation/product -> SBIR/STTR;
- high-risk research mechanism -> academic EAGER/standard program;
- DOE-mission scientific computing/instrumentation -> matching Office of Science FOA.

Never rewrite the evidence status to fit a solicitation.
