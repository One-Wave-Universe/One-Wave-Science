# Budget Framework

**Status:** planning scaffold; actual costs must be quoted for each proposal.

## Budget categories

### Personnel
Potential roles:
- project lead / PI-equivalent;
- experimental electronics engineer or technician;
- computational scientist / numerical methods contributor;
- research software engineer;
- part-time data/reproducibility support;
- external domain consultant(s) for specific tests.

### Equipment
Potential grant-justified equipment:
- calibrated oscilloscope or scope upgrade;
- function/arbitrary waveform generator;
- quality bench power supply;
- precision DMM;
- low-noise differential probes;
- ADC evaluation hardware;
- Jetson/edge compute accessories;
- isolated storage / backup;
- prototype PCB fabrication and assembly tools.

### Materials and supplies
- MOSFETs and switching devices;
- op-amp/comparator/interface parts where controls require them;
- passive component kits;
- coils/magnetics;
- sensors;
- breadboards/protoboards/PCBs;
- connectors and cabling;
- shielding/enclosures;
- calibration components.

### Software / compute
Prefer open-source tooling where practical. Budget only for services that materially improve reproducibility, storage, simulation, or collaboration.

### External services
Potential items:
- PCB fabrication/assembly;
- calibration services;
- machine shop or enclosure work;
- independent measurement/validation;
- specialist consulting;
- conference or dissemination costs.

## Budget logic by phase

- **Phase 0–1:** mostly personnel/software/reproducibility.
- **Phase 2:** instrumentation and calibration.
- **Phase 3–4:** electronics, fabrication, measurement time.
- **Phase 5:** domain data/computation/consultation.
- **Phase 6:** documentation, external review, dissemination.

## Cost-control rule

Do not buy specialized hardware until the preceding go/no-go gate shows that its measurement capability is required.
