# Preliminary Results and Gaps

## What can currently be presented as preliminary infrastructure

Subject to final verification before proposal submission, the repository appears to contain:

- a developed Virtual Breadboard simulation stack;
- prior SPICE-parity and convergence work;
- explicit state-machine and CELL_V1 architecture;
- V1 hypothesis chapters;
- a formal verification matrix;
- associative-memory prototype code;
- a proposed Wave Reader specification;
- repository-wide evidence-status and anti-drift rules.

These are preliminary **infrastructure and architecture results**, not proof of the new physics claims.

## What should not yet be presented as demonstrated

Do not describe the following as established without new evidence receipts:

- physical continuous superfluid/crystal lattice;
- ATP as a lattice phase shift;
- pleasure/pain as universal compression/expansion states;
- biological/quasar isomorphism;
- CELL_V1 magnetic memory/reinjection;
- Wave Reader performance before qualification;
- Boltzmann Administrator superiority;
- novel physical behavior from Hopfield melody cells.

## Highest-value missing evidence

1. One reproducible BASIS control-vs-hypothesis run.
2. One calibrated Wave Reader known-signal capture.
3. One frozen CELL_V1 control measurement.
4. One hypothesis measurement under the same procedure.
5. One independently rerunnable result package.
6. One external technical collaborator or reviewer.

## Grant readiness threshold

The repo becomes materially stronger for competitive experimental funding when it can show:

- at least one clean preliminary figure;
- one verified measurement chain;
- one bounded experiment with a preregistered success/failure criterion;
- one independent technical collaborator;
- a realistic budget and timeline tied to milestones.
