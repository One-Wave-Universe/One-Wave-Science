# NO ENTANGLEMENT

This is a One Wave rule, not a mood.

There is **no entanglement** in this science.

Two things affect each other only if they share a **connection you can point at**:

- a wire
- a CENTER / (0) node
- a current
- a field on a path that exists on the bench
- a receipt / packet that was actually passed

If you cannot name the net, it is not in the circuit.

Mirrors are opposite gates on one loop.
Hexes are wired neighbors.
Field and void are polarity on that loop, not a pair in the void.

Do not import spooky pairing, remote collapse-as-control, or "the other side knows without a net."
One wave. Local connection. Choice at a fence. Hold on the magnet.

Falsifier: a claimed effect with no named net, no current, no receipt. That claim is rejected here.
