# Mythology

Status: FICTION / MYTHIC CANON — not a scientific claim

This folder holds the One-Wave **mythology** — the narrative, cosmological, and moral story-world that borrows its structural language (Mirror Gate, compression/expression, 3:1/6:1/12:1/24:1, Point→Path→Field) from the One-Wave science repository, but is not itself part of that science.

## Why this is separated

The rest of this repository (`Nodes/`, `Books/`, `Governance_I_Series/`, `Internal_Proofs/`) is a claim-graded scientific framework: every node carries a gate (`GREEN`/`YELLOW`/`BRONZE`/etc.) under `Governance_I_Series/I-06_Canonical_Node_Metadata_and_Alias_Resolution.md`, and claims are expected to be backed by derivation or explicitly marked speculative.

The mythology needs room to go much further than that — angels, the afterlife, vampires, galactic war — without those ideas leaking into the science repository's claim graph as if they were derived results. Keeping mythology in its own top-level folder means:

- Nothing here carries a node ID, a gate, or I-06 metadata.
- Nothing here is referenced as evidence by any `Nodes/` or `Internal_Proofs/` file.
- The science can stay honest about what it hasn't demonstrated; the myth can go as far as the story needs.

## Science / myth boundary

The mythology's structural borrowings from the science side (compression/expression, the Mirror Gate, the 3→6→12→24 ladder, Point→Path→Field) are legitimate shared vocabulary — the science repository actually investigates these as mechanics.

Everything below the line is mythology only, and stays mythology unless the science repository someday derives independent support for it:

- Consciousness surviving biological death
- The 13th Angel
- Hell as colonized Void infrastructure
- Heaven as 24:1 integration
- Conscious 24:1 anchors
- Harmonic vampires
- Dreamspace as a traversable external substrate
- Soul transfer into android bodies
- After-death Mega City
- Void-energy wars
- Conscious beings physically stabilizing the universe
