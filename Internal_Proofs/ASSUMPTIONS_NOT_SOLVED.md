# Assumptions, not solutions

Nothing here is solved. The Standard Model column does not get to be solved by being older.

## Open assumptions

A1. Three vortex phases, one closed loop.
A2. Equal spacing, 120 degrees.
A3. The mass gap sits on the boundary.
B1. Three quark constituents plus gluon exchange.
B2. Confinement follows from QCD as a theorem.
B3. The mass gap is gluon-field energy.

A3 and B3 are the same kind of assignment. Neither is a proof.

## Already measured, not an assumption

Proton mass 938.272 MeV. Outer radius used by Chapter 2, 0.8409 fm. Inner 0.7331 fm. H1 peak in the fetched window, 4.56e-19. CMS 24446 is an 8 TeV pp record, hits not loaded.

## First proof step, no gap inserted

Geometry only:

R+ / R- = 0.7331 / 0.8409 = 0.872
lambda = 2 pi R- = 5.284 fm
A = 8.886 fm^2

sigma_T is not computed in this step. Computing it would use A3.

## Kill conditions

A1 dies if a stable one-phase or two-phase loop meets the same radius with the same update rule.
A2 dies if the form-factor split needs a spacing other than 120 degrees.
A3 dies if the neutron shell needs a second surface tension.
B2 stays unproved until an analytic confinement proof exists. Absence of that proof is not evidence for A1.
