# Rabbit Hopping — Chapter 3
# Complexity, Balance, and Aperiodicity

A binary trace is not Sturmian merely because it looks irregular.

The defining complexity law for an infinite Sturmian word is:

[
p(m)=m+1.
]

That means the number of distinct length-`m` factors is exactly one more than `m`.

A second important property is **balance**: two factors of equal length differ in their number of 1s by at most one.

The third is **aperiodicity**: the word is not generated by endlessly repeating one finite block.

For finite Rabbit-Hop receipts we cannot prove an infinite property from a short run. We therefore record:
- sample length;
- factor lengths tested;
- observed factor complexity;
- balance results;
- period-search results;
- empirical token frequency.

If the test range fails, the exact Sturmian label fails. Do not fix the sequence afterward to make the name fit.
