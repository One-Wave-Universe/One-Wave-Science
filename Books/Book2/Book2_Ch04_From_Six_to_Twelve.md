# Book 2 — Chapter 4: From Six to Twelve

The architecture repeatedly encounters six-direction and twelve-direction structures. The danger is seeing the same numbers in several places and treating recurrence as proof.

The current structural ladder is

```
3:1 <-> 1(0)1 <-> 1:6
6:1 <-> 1(0)1 <-> 1:12
12:1 <-> 1(0)1 <-> 1:24
```

The six routes of a 2D hexagonal cell are geometrically clear once the hexagon is chosen. What remains unproved is whether the underlying One-Wave dynamics **requires** that geometry, and whether extending the same physical rule into three dimensions necessarily gives twelve rather than merely assuming twelve.

The derivation therefore starts before the number. It defines degrees of freedom, mirrored orientations, the null/reference, symmetry equivalences, and only then counts the resulting unique states.

The same rule applies to the proposed **-6 to +12 wrapper range**. Before it can be physical, the endpoints must have an exact meaning and arise from the state space.

Rabbit Hop, triple flip, A/B/C geometry, PPF, and the Circle of Fifths can expose useful structural correspondences. They are cross-checks and clues, not substitutes for derivation.

Canonical science node: **G-768**.
