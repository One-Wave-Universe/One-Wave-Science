## Completion gate for every chapter

A chapter is not publication-ready merely because prose exists. It must have:

1. a Gray/control section that states established reference science;
2. at least one dimensionally meaningful equation where the subject is quantitative;
3. definitions for every symbol introduced;
4. at least one figure/graph when geometry, dynamics, or comparison is central;
5. a One-Wave mapping separated from the control model;
6. explicit predictions/tests;
7. a Yellow Audit naming what is not established;
8. links to raw data/simulation receipts when evidence is claimed.
