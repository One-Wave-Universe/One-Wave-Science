## 1. Core architecture

Algorythm-Zer0 separates three independent axes:

- **X — Control:** what operation is occurring?
- **Y — Depth:** how much mathematical structure is represented?
- **Z — Structure:** what rotational / structural form exists?

Any X level may coexist with any Y and Z level. Matching level numbers do not mean the axes are identical.

```text
address = (X,Y,Z)
legal address space = 6 × 6 × 6 = 216
```

FIELD means **active interactions**.  
VOID means **unexpressed potential**.  
The shared reference is the comparison / balance relation, not a third committed polarity.

## 3. Y — Depth

The six depth forms are:

```text

→ DIFFERENTIAL

```

Each depth N has N Field primitives and N Void primitives. Depth is representational complexity, not Control and not physical scale.

## 6. Commitment model

The executable control model uses a bounded latent variable:

```text
q ∈ [-q_max,+q_max]
```

with five readout states:

```text
-3  FULL DISAGREE
-2  PARTIAL DISAGREE
 0  UNITY / UNCOMMITTED
+2  PARTIAL AGREE
+3  FULL AGREE
```

±1 are intentionally unused.

Reference hysteresis thresholds:

```text
partial_exit  = 0.28
partial_enter = 0.40
full_exit     = 0.68
full_enter    = 0.82
```

These are executable reference defaults, not universal physical constants.
