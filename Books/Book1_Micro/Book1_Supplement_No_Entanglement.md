## The mistake this chapter removes

A wave is not a pair of ghosts. When it hits a detector or a lens in the eye, a plus or a minus is read. A brain or a computer can keep those peaks and troughs and walk them back along the path that actually ran. That is how distance and placement are known.

There is no entanglement here. Connection is a net you can point at.
