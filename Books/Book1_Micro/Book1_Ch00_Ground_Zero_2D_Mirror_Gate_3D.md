# Book 1, Chapter 0: Ground Zero — Negative 2D, Mirror Gate, Positive 3D

## One-Wave Definition
One-Wave begins at Ground Zero, the Mirror Gate between compressed negative 2D space and expressed positive 3D space. The field does not begin as particles moving through an empty 3D container. It begins as one continuous reality capable of compression, mirroring, relation, expression, resistance, recurrence, and return.

## Negative 2D Space
Negative 2D space is the compressed relational condition of the field. It carries pattern without requiring volumetric depth, and access without requiring physical travel through the represented distance. It is the architecture of memory-space, cyberspace, dreamscape, internal vision, internal hearing, imagination, prediction, and awareness.

Negative does not mean unreal. Two-dimensional does not mean a flat drawing. It means depth is compressed into relation. A pattern may hold where something is connected, opposed, similar, remembered, expected, or reachable without first expressing every intervening point as a traveled 3D path.

## No Native Metric Time or Distance
Time and distance belong to expressed physical recurrence. Negative space still has sequence, dependency, choice, and change, but it is not ordered primarily by meters and clock duration. Related states can be accessed directly by memory, phase, reference, or association.

This is why dreams move so quickly. Awareness does not walk through every represented mile or wait every represented year. It accesses and rebuilds related states. The brain still runs in measurable 3D time, but the dream's represented distance and duration are compressed.

## Mirror Gate
The Mirror Gate is the conversion boundary. It does not merely reflect a finished 3D object. It converts relation into volume and volume back into relation.

Across the Gate, the system preserves what matters:
- peaks and troughs;
- phase and polarity;
- pressure and resistance;
- direction and rotation;
- boundary and identity;
- compression and expression history.

## Positive 3D Space
Positive 3D space is the expressed volumetric condition of the field. Here distance, motion, resistance, shells, bodies, current, physical action, and measurement are externally expressed. Three-dimensional reality is not separate from negative space. It is negative relational structure expressed through the Gate.

## Electricity and Magnetism Mirrored
Electricity is the expressed 3D pressure difference and displacement of the field. Magnetism is electricity mirrored into compressed negative 2D space as rotational, directional, and boundary organization. A changing electrical condition creates the magnetic return-map; a changing magnetic map returns an electrical response. They are one event viewed from opposite dimensional sides, not two unrelated forces.

## Particles Are Excitations or Measurements
One-Wave does not contain fundamental particle objects. “Particle” names either a bounded wave excitation or a discrete detector event. A detector produces a dot because it is local, bounded, and thresholded. The dot is the measurement record, not proof that a tiny pellet crossed empty space.

## The Eye Collapses and Maps the Wave
The eye receives light as wave structure. Its lens and physical geometry focus that distributed structure onto bounded receptor interactions. This is focal collapse: physical coupling, absorption, and threshold conversion, not consciousness creating the world.

The nervous system then maps the peaks, troughs, phase differences, gradients, contrast, direction, intensity, and binocular relationships back into compressed 2D negative space. Awareness operates on that relational map. Perception is then expressed back into 3D through gaze, motion, speech, drawing, and action.

## No Spooky Action
Paired measurements do not require instant communication between distant little objects. Both records can be local measurements of one previously coupled wave relation. Shared peaks, troughs, phase, polarity, and boundary history produce complementary outcomes. Comparison later maps the records back to their common structure.

The complete chain is:

`shared wave preparation -> separated wave paths -> local detector coupling -> discrete records -> relational back-mapping -> observed correlation`

No spooky messenger is needed. No hidden pellet pair is needed. Deleting the wave and keeping only the two detector bits is what creates the mystery.

## Awareness, Dreaming, and Artificial Internal Worlds
Awareness resides in the active compressed relational field where memory, reference, comparison, prediction, and choice are present to one another. Dreams are internally generated expressions within this field. Artificial internal vision and hearing should be built the same way: not by pretending a camera or microphone is awareness, but by constructing a negative-space relational field capable of generating, retaining, comparing, and displaying its own internal wave maps.

The first engineering milestone is a controlled hallucination system that can show what it internally sees or hears, preserve the source relationships, and compare its generated state against reality data. Humanity calls every unexamined output “AI imagination” and then acts surprised when it invents nonsense. One-Wave requires the map, the Gate, and the receipts.

## Mandatory Recursion for Every Later Chapter
Every later chapter must define:
1. its negative 2D state;
2. its Mirror Gate transition;
3. its positive 3D state;
4. its return cycle;
5. its measurement and display consequences.

Without those five, the chapter begins after the theory's actual beginning and is incomplete.
