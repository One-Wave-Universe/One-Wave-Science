# Node supply — Book 1, gravity and electromagnetism

Sources: A-115, C-311, C-319, C-320, D-409. Not a replacement for Ch12 or Ch13.

Gravity is the restoring gradient of the compression field. chi = -div u. g0 = -alpha grad chi. Magnetism does not replace that.

C-311: electric and magnetic are one pressure. Electric is the radial part, E ~ grad P_c. Magnetic is the rotational part, B ~ curl P_c.

C-319: a rotational magnetic state reorganizes lattice paths. C-320: g = -alpha K_L grad chi, with K_L = I + kappa_R R. Off returns the baseline. kappa_R is not set.

D-409: the native lattice is three-dimensional close pack. One center, twelve neighbors. A flat hex is a slice.

Displacement is what energy does on that lattice: potential chi, slope grad chi, then point, path, and field rates. Missing one of the three rates leaves the node incomplete (ROTATIONS3).
Magnetism opens the point. Closed, it resists. That is the turn. Gravity does not affect point rotation (G-749, C-319, Ch13).
