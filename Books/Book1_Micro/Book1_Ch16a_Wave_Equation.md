Companion to Ch16 (No Entanglement) and Node A-110a.

Memory of motion (A-109) is μ ∂²ψ/∂t².

Set them equal:

```text
∂²ψ/∂t² = v² ∂²ψ/∂x²,    v² = α/μ
```
