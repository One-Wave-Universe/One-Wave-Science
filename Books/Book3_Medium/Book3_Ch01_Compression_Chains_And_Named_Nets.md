## Gray — Standard Model Reference

Conventional physiology describes an organism as: cells organized into
tissues, tissues into organs, organs into systems, coordinated by nervous
and hormonal signaling. At the metabolic level, cells break down sugars
and fats (glycolysis, the citric acid cycle, oxidative phosphorylation)
to regenerate ATP from ADP and inorganic phosphate. ATP hydrolysis
releases roughly 7.3 kcal/mol under standard conditions and drives everything
from muscle contraction to ion pumping to biosynthesis.

Standard Model strength: a detailed, well-instrumented account of specific
enzymes, membrane potentials, and reaction pathways, each independently
verified.
Standard Model limitation: no attempt to connect this metabolic bookkeeping
to the same update rule governing lattice dynamics at other scales — chemistry
and physics remain administratively separate literatures even where the
same organism spans both.

## 2D One-Wave Interpretation

In One-Wave terms (B-228), a sugar or fat chain is a stored **Expression** —
lean not yet spent. Digestion is stepwise **Compression**: enzymes and
membrane potential drive the chain into a tight well, piece by piece.
The **Release / Return** step hands off a small reusable packet — ATP —
so another loop elsewhere can move. **Hold** is the packet sitting unspent,
charge on a wall, until a named enzyme spends it.

```text
chain --compress--> well --packet--> ATP --spend--> motion / pump / hold elsewhere
```

This is bookkeeping over real chemistry, not a replacement for it. B-228 is
explicit: sugars and fats are real carbon chains, ATP is a real molecule,
the mitochondrion is a real organelle with real membranes. The One-Wave
layer only names which move (compress / hold / release) a given chemical
step corresponds to.

## 3D One-Wave Interpretation

B-229 extends the same three-move shape (B-223: Left / Stay / Right, here
read as down / hold / up across a gradient) across every organism scale on
its own named net:

| Scale | Unit | Named net the loop runs on |
|---|---|---|
| micro | one reaction, one channel | a single membrane, a single gradient |
| small | one cell | the cell's own membrane potential |
| cluster | tissue | gap junctions / shared extracellular space |
| body | organ systems | nerve and hormone pathways |
| macro | organism in a place | the whole organism's sensed environment |

The claim is explicitly not that a nucleus is a small universe — it is that
the same signed-ternary engagement structure (B-223/B-224: engage or don't,
then left/stay/right if engaged) recurs at each scale, on a different named
physical wire each time. "Two kidneys, two sides of a synapse: they work
because they share a body. That body is the net." (B-229). Per C-324, there
is no coordination claimed between organs, or between the two kidneys, that
does not run over a traceable nerve, hormone, or shared-fluid pathway. A
coordination claim with no named net behind it is not licensed by this
chapter.

## Mathematics

The only formal structure this chapter imports is B-223's differential rule,
applied to a biological gradient `Delta` (a membrane potential, a proton
gradient, a phosphate-transfer rate) around a measured zero window `epsilon`:

```text
Delta < -epsilon  -> -1  (down / release)
|Delta| <= epsilon -> 0  (hold)
Delta > +epsilon  -> +1  (up / compress)
```

No scale-transition function is claimed here. B-220 (Scale Layer) is explicit
that no derived γ(s)/β(s) scaling law exists connecting Micro through Macro
in general — this chapter does not attempt to derive one for the metabolic
case either. It only asserts that the same three-way test applies at each
named scale in the table above, independently, not that one formula predicts
how the test's parameters change from scale to scale.

No kcal/ATP energy value is derived here. B-228 states this directly: the
sketch is bookkeeping, not a derivation of the 7.3 kcal/mol figure.

## Predictions

1. A phosphate-transfer event (ATP <-> ADP + Pi) measured against a fixed
   threshold should sort cleanly into the same three states — hold, spend,
   recharge — as B-223's differential rule, with no fourth state required.
2. The same three-move test (down/hold/up around a measured zero window)
   should apply, independently and without a shared formula, to a single
   ion channel, a single neuron's membrane potential, and a whole organ's
   net secretory rate. Failure to find a clean three-way sort at any one of
   these scales would count against B-229's claim that the shape recurs.
3. Any apparent coordination between two organs (e.g., paired kidneys, or
   two sides of a synapse) should trace to an identifiable shared nerve,
   hormone, or fluid-space net. Per C-324 and B-229's own falsifier, a
   coordination claim that cannot be traced to a named net is not supported
   by this framework and should be treated as a gap, not evidence for it.
