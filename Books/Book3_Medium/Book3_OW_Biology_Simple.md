# One Wave Biology — simple

Node B-229. Yellow. Leaves the silicon cell on the bench.

A living thing is not a mystery fluid. It is membranes (fences), chains (food), packets (ATP), gates (channels), and receipts (copy rules).

Eat = bring a chain in.
Digest = cut the chain.
Compress = squeeze the pieces through a well on a membrane.
Packet = ATP, a hold another loop can spend.
Move or think = spend the packet across another fence.
Hold = tone, charge on a wall, phosphate not yet spent.

Two eyes, two kidneys, two sides of a synapse: they work because they share a body. That body is the net. Nothing is paired in the void.

Nerves: stay until the lean crosses T, then a spike, then return. Same three moves as the hex cell, in salt water.

That is the whole simplification. Add a molecule only when you can point at it.
