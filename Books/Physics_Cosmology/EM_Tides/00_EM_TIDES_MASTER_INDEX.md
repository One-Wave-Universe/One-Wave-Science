# EM, Rotation, Tides, and Spin-Orbit Physics

## Purpose
This module expands One-Wave Science with a falsifiable bridge between electromagnetic rotation, material dissipation, tides, spin-orbit evolution, planetary dynamos, magnetospheres, and technology.

## Status labels
- Observation: measured behavior.
- Standard model: established quantitative account.
- One-Wave hypothesis: proposed deeper mechanism.
- Required derivation: equation, simulation, or experiment needed.
- Falsifier: result that rules out the hypothesis.

1. `01_ESTABLISHED_EM_AND_TIDAL_BASELINES.md` — Maxwell, Lorentz force, induction, tidal torque, conservation.
2. `02_ONE_WAVE_ROTATION_HYPOTHESES.md` — rotational phase-field proposals and strict limits.
3. `03_TIDAL_LOCKING_AND_SPIN_ORBIT.md` — lagged deformation, synchronous rotation, resonances.

5. `05_PREDICTIONS_AND_FALSIFICATION.md` — claim ledger and controls.

## Non-negotiable rule
Do not merge an observation, its standard explanation, and a One-Wave proposal into a single claim. Every claimed coupling must include conservation accounting, baseline reproduction, a null configuration, polarity or phase reversal where applicable, a scaling law, uncertainty estimates, and a disproof condition.
