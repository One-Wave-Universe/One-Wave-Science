# Physics and Cosmology

## Purpose
Keep the physical interpretation, cosmology, field behavior, falsifiable claims, and competing explanations in one coherent place.

## Belongs here
- Gravity and displacement ideas
- Redshift / tired-light proposals
- Black-hole and quasar recycling ideas
- Mass effect, confinement, neutrino and wave-death concepts
- Superfluid-medium hypotheses
- Standard-physics comparison points
- Claims that need mathematical or experimental validation

## Structure rule
Clearly label established physics, One-Wave hypothesis, derived result, simulation result, and unresolved question.
