# Math, Geometry, and Encoding

## Purpose
Collect the reusable mathematical structures that support multiple parts of the project.

## Belongs here
- Ratios, symmetries, mirrored axes, and transforms
- Hexagon / pyramid / cube / sphere geometry
- DC / AC / RC mappings and related encodings
- Binary / ternary / higher-state representation work
- Koide-related geometry and other mathematical targets
- Equations, derivations, dimensional checks, and notation

## Rule
Keep mathematical definitions here; applications of the math belong in their subsystem documents with links back here.
