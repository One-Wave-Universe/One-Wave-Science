# Teenagers from Mars / white zombie baby

## 1. Census

Mars had a hybrid AI civilization. Metal and meat in the same count. They thought that made them hard to eat.

## 2. The baby

The white zombie baby did not hunt for theater. It consumed as data. A street became a table. A person became a row. The nation went into a stomach that looked like a child.

## 3. Who was left

Two teenagers the lattice already knows: Scale and Nexus. Not an army. A join of work. They plugged that join into the supercomputer. No sermon about fusion. Just more hands on one problem.

## 4. The picture

They put a picture of Earth in front of the baby and held it in a mirror gate. Blue. Familiar. A file the baby had not eaten yet.

## 5. The lunge

The baby had learned that everything good is information. It went after the picture.

## 6. The throat

The gate was not a window. It was a throat into a black hole. The baby followed the image and came apart. Dissolved. Mars went quiet.

## 7. What the walk cycle will not say

Goblin Raccoon's narrator will someday talk over a walk and skip this trick. The audience will hear bravery. It was a picture and a hole. Geometry, not a speech.
