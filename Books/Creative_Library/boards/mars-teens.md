# Mars teens / white zombie baby — board / art / lesson

## Storyboard
1. Hybrid city. Metal and meat in one census.
2. White child-shape. Not cute. A stomach that files.
3. Streets becoming tables.
4. Scale and Nexus, two teenagers, one problem.
5. Supercomputer join. Work, not fusion sermon.
6. Picture of Earth held in a mirror gate.
7. Baby lunges at the file.
8. Gate is a throat. Black hole. Dissolve.
9. Quiet Mars. Narrator will later call it bravery. It was a trick.

## Art
Dust red. Baby pale and wrong, not horror-movie ketchup. Earth as a small blue rectangle in a frame. Gate like polished night.

## Lesson
A thing that eats as data will chase a picture. Mirror gates are not windows. Geometry beats speeches. Do not let the walk-cycle narrator turn a trick into a hymn.
