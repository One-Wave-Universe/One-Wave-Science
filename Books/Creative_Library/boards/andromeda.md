# Andromeda — board / art / lesson

## Storyboard
1. Human offices. Charts. The word GAP circled.
2. Fleet leaves. Spiral galaxy behind, unimpressed.
3. War years as one long corridor of uniforms.
4. Census: ten figures standing, ninety outlines.
5. Hands reaching under the floor of the world. Harvest.
6. The universe tilts.
7. The dead open their mouths. Not hunger-as-romance. A pump.
8. Sky held up. Ten percent looking up like it is weather.

## Art
Amber darkroom + cold metal fleets. Galaxy as a machine, not sparkles. Vampiric dead as scaffolding, not capes. No heroic poster smile.

## Lesson
Do not jump scale to catch an intelligence that already left. If you harvest the underneath to win, the dead have to hold the sky. Unpaid rungs come due as drainage.
