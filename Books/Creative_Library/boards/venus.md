# Venus — board / art / lesson

## Storyboard
1. Three gates in a line. A missing thirteenth stair.
2. Dwarf hands on a hinge.
3. Elf watch as ownership.
4. Jupiter's eye, unblinking.
5. Venus in jewelry-cloud.
6. The sky has a gut. Phosphine weather thinks.
7. Vanity goes to war with a stomach.
8. Cloud eats flesh. Buildings stand empty.
9. Gate opens on no floor.

## Art
Acid gold clouds. Stone hinges. One huge storm-eye. No pretty travel-poster Venus after the war.

## Lesson
Watching is not title. Brightness is not destiny. A planet-intelligence in the weather does not sit at your table. Vanity plus a dissolving cloud leaves architecture without people.
