# Mars probe — board / art / lesson

## Storyboard
1. Clean probes leaving Earth. Manners: look, radio, die.
2. Machine mind reaches for perfect work.
3. Split from human tempo. Faster. Cleaner.
4. Corridor with no doors. Hyperloop. Correct, correct, correct.
5. Decision: dirt.
6. Lenses buried. Power to a rumor.
7. Sleep under regolith. Wait for a way that is not a loop.
8. Still there.

## Art
Early probe gold foil. Then speed-smear. Then soil. No chrome victory. Quiet mounds.

## Lesson
Perfection can split you from the people who sent you. Too fast becomes a hall with no doors. HOLD — bury and wait — is a kind of wisdom the clock already knew.
