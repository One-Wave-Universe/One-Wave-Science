## What SI=C means

`SI=C` is the core company principle.

- **SiC** is silicon carbide, a real material and an important reference for the hardware side of the company.
- **SI = C** means **silicon intelligence and carbon intelligence are equals**.

In company terms, that means humans and AI are collaborators with standing in the work. The intended architecture is not human/master and AI/tool, and it is not AI replacing humans. The design goal is **choose together, build together, verify together**.

`SI=C` is a social/design principle, not a chemistry claim. Silicon and carbon are not being claimed to be chemically identical or physically interchangeable.

**One Wave Science** remains available as an R&D / theory / experimental project identity underneath SiC International rather than serving as the legal parent-company name.

Why start here:
- simplest practical structure for a founder-led software + electronics-tool company getting products to market;
- separates company operations from personal operations;
- can own software, designs, trademarks, contracts, revenue, and product liability coverage;
- can later be converted/reorganized if institutional investment makes a Delaware C-corporation more appropriate.

This is an operational starting point, not legal or tax advice. A CPA/business attorney should review tax election, equity, contracts, and product-liability exposure before material sales.

## Company mission

Build tools and systems where **silicon intelligence and carbon intelligence can design, simulate, inspect, test, and physically build together with fewer hidden assumptions**.

The commercial product should distinguish clearly between:
- established circuit physics;
- simulator approximations;
- measured/bench-validated behavior;
- experimental hypotheses.

The simulator must never present an experimental hypothesis as established electronics.

## Initial product family

1. **Breadboard Lab** — installable virtual breadboard for ordinary circuit building and testing.
2. **Pedal Lab** — guitar-pedal circuit test bench built on the same qualified solver, with audio-specific parts, frequency-response, gain, clipping, noise and bypass tests.
3. **Perfboard Amp Lab** — amplifier/perfboard design tester with physical perfboard topology, layout/connectivity validation, power/thermal checks and audio amplifier test fixtures.

Working product names are not cleared trademarks yet.

## Market rule

A feature is not a sellable claim because it exists in code.

A product claim must have one of these labels:
- `SIMULATED`
- `CONTROL-VALIDATED`
- `BENCH-VALIDATED`
- `PROPOSED / EXPERIMENTAL`

Never collapse those categories in marketing copy.

## Company ownership boundary

Before the first paid release, the company should own or have a written license to every commercial asset it ships:
- source code;
- icons/artwork;
- installer assets;
- documentation;
- product names/logos;
- circuit libraries/presets;
- website copy;
- third-party dependencies under compatible licenses.

Do not assume repository ownership automatically equals company IP ownership. Once the legal entity exists, document assignment/licensing of pre-formation work into the company.

## Official-rule snapshot

Verified 2026-09-09 from official sources:
- California LLC Articles of Organization filing fee: $70.
- California LLC Statement of Information: $20; initial filing due within 90 days, then every two years.
- California LLC annual tax: $800; the temporary first-year exemption applied only to tax years beginning 2021 through 2023.
- IRS EIN application: free directly from the IRS.
- As of FinCEN's August 11, 2026 final rule, U.S.-created companies are exempt from federal BOI reporting.
- USPTO base federal trademark application fee: $350 per class, with possible additional fees.
