# California LLC Formation Checklist — SiC International

Status: preparation checklist only. Filing has not been completed from this repository.

## 1. Name clearance

Working legal name: `SiC International LLC`.

Primary working mark: `SI=C`.

Before filing/launch:
- search California bizfile for identical/confusing entity names;
- search USPTO trademarks for `SIC INTERNATIONAL`, `SI=C`, and close variants in relevant software/electronics classes;
- search web/domain/app-store usage;
- keep product names provisional until clearance is done.

A general web search is not a substitute for the California entity-name check or federal trademark search.

## 2. Information needed for Articles of Organization

Prepare:
- final LLC legal name;
- California business mailing address;
- principal office address;
- agent for service of process name/address (individual or registered corporate agent);
- management choice: member-managed or manager-managed;
- organizer information;
- effective filing date if not immediate.

Do not put private addresses or identity numbers into this public repository.

## 3. File Articles of Organization

California Secretary of State filing fee verified 2026-09-09: **$70**.

Use California Secretary of State bizfile Online or the current official filing route.

Save the accepted filing and entity number in private company records, not in the public repo if it exposes sensitive information.

## 4. Statement of Information

Current California requirement verified 2026-09-09:
- initial Statement of Information due within **90 days** after registration;
- filing fee **$20**;
- LLC Statements of Information recur every **two years**.

Put the recurring due date on the company calendar immediately after formation.

## 5. California Franchise Tax Board

Current California rule verified 2026-09-09:
- annual LLC tax: **$800**;
- generally due by the 15th day of the fourth month from formation for the first taxable year, then on the applicable annual schedule;
- the first-year exemption applied only to tax years beginning on/after 2021 and before 2024, so do not budget as if the exemption still exists;
- additional LLC fees begin when California income exceeds $250,000.

Get tax-election/accounting advice before electing S-corporation treatment or changing the default federal tax classification.

## 6. EIN

After the LLC is legally formed:
- apply directly through the IRS;
- EIN is **free**;
- do not pay a third-party site merely to obtain an EIN;
- save the IRS confirmation in private company records.

## 7. Federal BOI reporting

As of FinCEN's August 11, 2026 final rule, **U.S.-created companies are exempt from BOI reporting**.

This rule has changed more than once historically, so re-check FinCEN before relying on this file in a later year.

## 8. Operating agreement

Before money, collaborators, or ownership becomes complicated, execute a written operating agreement covering at least:
- owner/member identity and percentage;
- management authority;
- capital contributions;
- distributions;
- voting/major decisions;
- adding/removing members;
- IP ownership;
- what happens if a member leaves, dies, becomes incapacitated, or wants to sell an interest;
- dissolution.

Keep signed documents private.

## 9. Founder IP assignment

Pre-formation software/design work does not magically move into the LLC just because the company is formed.

Execute a written assignment or license that clearly identifies the assets being transferred, including the One-Wave-Science repository and SiC International commercial product assets where appropriate.

Do not assign third-party code/assets the founder does not own.

## 10. Banking/accounting

After entity + EIN:
- company bank account;
- payment processor account;
- bookkeeping ledger;
- separate company expenses from personal expenses;
- retain receipts/contracts/invoices;
- choose fiscal/tax handling with tax professional.

## 11. Local permits

Check the city/county where the company is actually operated for:
- business license/business tax certificate;
- home-occupation rules if applicable;
- sales-tax/seller's permit requirements if selling taxable physical products;
- any special rules for assembly/manufacturing.

Do not guess the local jurisdiction from this repository.
