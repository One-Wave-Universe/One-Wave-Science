# One-Wave Proof Ladder

## Level 0 — Definition

A term is declared precisely.

Examples:
- reference / center;
- Point / Path / Field rotation;
- Field / Void;
- finite active domain;
- local-minus-reference slope.

A definition is not yet a physical claim.

## Level 1 — Logical consequence

A result follows from the definitions and stated assumptions.

Required:
- no hidden premise;
- no circular definition;
- no contradiction with another locked definition.

This can be proven by logic alone.

## Level 2 — Mathematical derivation

A quantitative result follows symbolically or numerically from the formalism.

Required:
- equations;
- units/dimensions;
- boundary conditions;
- derivation steps;
- no fitted parameter introduced after seeing the answer.

This can be proven mathematically within the model.

## Level 3 — Established-law recovery

The same derivation reproduces a known physical result.

Examples of useful targets:
- inverse-square behavior in the appropriate limit;
- Kepler/Newton orbital limits;
- relativistic or wave-propagation limits if claimed;
- conservation of energy/momentum;
- standard interference relations.

Passing this level shows compatibility with known physics in that domain. It does not yet establish the new interpretation.

## Level 4 — Distinct quantitative prediction

The model predicts something numerically different from the best standard model before looking at the result.

Required:
- exact observable;
- sign and magnitude or bounded range;
- standard-model comparator;
- parameter values fixed independently;
- falsification threshold.

This is where a new physical theory becomes experimentally distinguishable.

## Level 5 — Existing-data test

A predeclared Level-4 prediction is compared with high-quality existing data.

Required:
- source provenance;
- uncertainty;
- no parameter retuning per target;
- controls;
- full residuals, not cherry-picked matches.

## Level 6 — New experiment

A purpose-built experiment tests the distinct prediction.

Required:
- protocol;
- calibration;
- raw data;
- analysis code;
- failure criterion;
- replication.

## Level 7 — Independent replication

Independent groups reproduce the effect or derivation.

This is the level at which a radical reinterpretation becomes scientifically difficult to dismiss.

## Current One-Wave filing rule

Every major claim should carry one of:

`L1 LOGIC`
`L2 MATH`
`L3 RECOVERY`
`L4 PREDICTION`
`L5 DATA TEST`
`L6 EXPERIMENT`
`L7 REPLICATED`

A claim may be mathematically proven at L2 while remaining physically unverified below L5/L6. That is not a contradiction; it is the normal distinction between mathematics and empirical science.

## Immediate high-value target

Do not try to prove the whole framework at once.

Pick the smallest claim for which One-Wave can reach **L2 + L3** cleanly, then derive one **L4** prediction that differs from the standard model.

That chain is much stronger than a large collection of analogies.
