## Nested epicycles

"Epicycle" means a real child rotation carried by a real parent rotation.

Every nested rotation must correspond to an independently identifiable physical parent/child level. No extra rotation may be inserted merely to improve a fit.

## Nonlocal three-body hypothesis

The target is not "there are no solutions." The test is whether three bodies are better represented as one simultaneous relational field state, with local trajectories read from the whole configuration.

Novelty requires something beyond coordinate relabeling: a new invariant, exact family, simplification/regularization, or distinct quantitative prediction.

Track point rotation, orbital/path rotation, internal differential rotation, intrinsic/induced magnetism, magnetic-axis tilt/offset, tidal relation, bound/unbound lattice state, and parent-field reference.

No private per-planet correction law is allowed.

## Solar corona program

## Carbon / Hoyle-state program

## Wave / detector program

The One-Wave "choice at interaction" interpretation becomes new physics only if it predicts a measurable difference from standard quantum optics.
