# Challenge 05 — Carbon Creation / Hoyle-State Derivation

**Target:** L2 nuclear-structure derivation -> L3 recovery of the Hoyle-state energy and triple-alpha behavior -> possible L4 prediction.

## Standard problem

Stellar carbon-12 is produced primarily through the triple-alpha process. The key resonance is the excited (0^+) Hoyle state of carbon-12 near the (^{8}{m Be}+alpha) threshold.

The basic carbon-production mechanism is established. The hard One-Wave target is therefore **not** "discover how stars make carbon." It is to derive the relevant carbon-12 resonance/cluster structure and reaction behavior from the framework rather than inserting the observed resonance.

## One-Wave question

Can the lattice/rotation/boundary formalism predict a three-alpha bound/resonant configuration whose energy, spin/parity structure, and decay behavior correspond to the Hoyle state?

## Minimum mathematical program

1. Represent three alpha clusters with one common interaction law.
2. Derive allowed symmetric configurations and rotational/vibrational modes.
3. Obtain a (0^+) excited resonance without inputting its measured energy.
4. Compute the resonance energy relative to the triple-alpha threshold.
5. Derive or approximate its width / lifetime and transition route to the carbon-12 ground-state band.
6. Use the derived resonance parameters in the triple-alpha reaction rate.
7. Compare the resulting carbon-production rate against standard nuclear-astrophysics calculations.

## Critical anti-fitting rule

The Hoyle-state energy must be a prediction from independently fixed model parameters. If the coupling is tuned to place the state at the observed energy, this is a fit, not a derivation.

## Strong next prediction

After reproducing the known Hoyle state, predict another measurable carbon-12 excitation, rotational-band property, branching ratio, or reaction-rate correction **before** using the corresponding data.

## Failure condition

If the framework cannot generate a near-threshold three-alpha resonance with the correct quantum numbers and scale without post-hoc tuning, it has not explained carbon creation.
