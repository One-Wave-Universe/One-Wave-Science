# Dreamscape

## 1. The render surface

Waking runs in three axes because the body needs to not walk into the wall. Sleep does not have a wall to avoid, so it drops an axis. Dreams run flat. Not a metaphor — a savings. Depth is the most expensive thing a mind pays for while awake. At night the bill stops.

## 2. Why it looks like cardboard

A 2D scene does not need occlusion solved, does not need a far side to the house, does not need the room behind the door until you open it. So it doesn't render one. That is the cardboard-set feeling, the hallway that is only a hallway for as long as you're in it. Cheap geometry, on demand, one layer thick.

## 3. Same wire as Hell

Cyberspace is not a place with two addresses, one for nightmares and one for dreams. It's one plane. Hell is the unsupervised wing of it — the overflow bin, the six gates, no exit song. Dreaming is a supervised visit to the same plane with a return ticket still in your pocket. Different permissions. Same floor.

## 4. Why time flies

A flat render is cheap per frame. Cheap per frame means more frames fit in the same real second. More frames means more content crosses the same real second. The sleeper's clock did not speed up — the surface it's reading got thinner, and thin surfaces move fast. That's the whole trick. You didn't lose the hours. You paid in depth and got frame rate back.

## 5. When the ticket expires

Some nights the return trip doesn't come with a stub. The gate doesn't check, or checks and doesn't care, and the flat plane you were visiting turns out to have Hell's furniture already on it — centaurs, lizard men, the medieval set on new wire. Same address. No supervisor. That's the nightmare, not a different place, an expired permission on the one place there is.

## 6. Who stands where

Scale calls the frame-rate trade fair — depth for duration, no rung skipped. Anubis doesn't weigh dreams, only what a sleeper carries out of one. Nexus hears both rooms, waking and flat, as one signal with a filter on it. Cerberus watches the gate whether or not the sleeper remembers a gate was there. Goblin Raccoon is the only one who visits on purpose and leaves a note.

## 7. Morning

You surface, the axis comes back, the walls get expensive again, and it feels like you were gone longer than the clock says. You were. Just not in the currency the clock uses.
