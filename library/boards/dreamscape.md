# Dreamscape — board / art / lesson

## Storyboard
1. Waking world, three axes, a wall you have to walk around.
2. Lights out. One axis drops. The room goes flat.
3. A hallway that is only a hallway while you're in it — no far side until you open the door.
4. Same wire pulled into frame: Hell's gate, glimpsed at the edge of the flat plane.
5. Frame-rate readout climbing as depth spends down to zero.
6. A ticket in a sleeper's pocket — the return trip, still stamped.
7. One night the stamp is missing. The flat plane already has Hell's furniture on it.
8. Morning: the axis snaps back, the walls get expensive again.

## Art
Two layers only, no vanishing point — dream geometry as a stage flat, hinge-thin, doors that are just doors. Cut to Hell's cable-and-stone only at the gate, never fully, to keep them one address with different lighting.

## Lesson
Sleep is not slower or faster time, it's cheaper rendering — depth traded for frame rate, more content per real second. Nightmares are not a different place, they're an expired permission on the same plane Hell already owns. Do not draw two cyberspaces where the story only has one.
