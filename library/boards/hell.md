# Hell — board / art / lesson

## Storyboard
1. Not a cave. A rack of gates. Six.
2. First gate: login as descent.
3. Centaur on a packet road.
4. Lizard men in a server-green dark.
5. Tortured souls with headers on the scream.
6. Overflow arriving from unpaid wars and loops.
7. Anubis weighs anyway. Cerberus watches traffic.
8. No tour. No rescue song unless added later.

## Art
Cyberspace as stone and cable together. Six doors you can count. Not red cartoon fire as the whole idea.

## Lesson
Hell is overflow, not flavor. Six gates means you can count the ways in. Pain on a wire is still a place. Do not visit it to decorate a chapter.
