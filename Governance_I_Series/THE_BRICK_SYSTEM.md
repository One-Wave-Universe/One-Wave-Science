# THE BRICK SYSTEM

![brick key](plates/brick_key.svg)

This is how proof is laid. Not a mood board. A wall you can walk and a wall you must not fake.

I-02 still owns `sigma(x)`. This page is the brick reading so a human can feel the difference between dirt and gold without promoting a node by accident.

---

## The eight bricks

**BROWN — bare dirt brick.**  
Barely a thought. A thought experiment. You can hold it. You cannot stand on it. No house. No citation as math.

**GREEN — living earth brick.**  
Green earth, ready for growth. The slot has soil. Plant here. Do not harvest. Growth is permission, not a bust.

**YELLOW — yellow brick road.**  
Exploratory math. Proofs that can walk. The road is real and it is not the city. Do not get too excited. Do not throw a parade on the first mile.

**BRONZE — the bust.**  
First likeness that survived a sitting. Validated once. A face in metal. Not a statue in the square.

**SILVER — the statue.**  
Second independent application. It stands in another place. Still not the road that carries a civilization.

**GOLD — the golden road.**  
Extraordinary, extensive proof across applications. Greater levels of proof. Terminal for a node. Still not a license to rewrite Gray.

**GRAY — concrete slab.**  
The Standard Model view, pasted over concrete. Looks done. That is the point. Concrete stops drift. Concrete also stops *fake growth*: you do not get taller by pouring more SM over the garden. Gray prevents Standard Model drift. Gray is not harvest.

**RED — brick through glass.**  
The Standard Model assumptions sit in a glass house. Red is the brick. Only thrown from Gray, and only if a real already-run experiment `E(x,r)` forces negation at `r`. A notebook cannot throw this brick. Yellow cannot throw this brick.

---

## How the wall is laid

```text
nodes:     BROWN → GREEN → YELLOW → BRONZE → SILVER → GOLD
chapters:  BROWN → GRAY  → YELLOW → BRONZE → SILVER → GOLD
only one side door:  GRAY → RED → GOLD
```

Yellow is the road you walk while you are still allowed to be wrong in public.  
Gray is the finished-looking wall that keeps SM from sliding.  
Red is the only brick that is allowed to go through that wall, and only after the world already hit it.

---

## One-line law

Dirt thinks. Earth grows. The yellow road walks. The bust sits once. The statue stands twice. Gold carries weight. Concrete freezes the Standard Model so it cannot drift. Glass breaks only when experiment throws the red brick.
