## Quantum Scale System: Electron Spin as PPF Center

### The Three Components at Electron Scale

**Point (P):** Electron spin magnetic moment ($\mu_e = e\hbar / 2m_e$)
- This is the center of PPF rotation at quantum scale
- Magnetic moment = spin angular momentum = stability marker

**Path (P):** Orbital angular momentum quantization around nucleus
- Electron can occupy discrete orbital shells (1s, 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, 3d...)
- Rabbit Hopping addressing: each shell is a signed offset from nuclear center
  - n=1 shell: K=-1 (tightly bound)
  - n=2 shell: K=+1 (first excited state)
  - n=3 shell: K=+2 (higher excited state)
- Path = the permitted routes through phase space (Rabbit Hop addressing)

**Field (F):** Electron cloud probability envelope
- Orbital envelope defines the field boundary
- Shape determined by spin-orbit coupling (magnetism reshaping field)
- Superposition states accessible during transitions

### Algorithm Zero at Electron Scale (Bohr Model + Spin)

```
BEGIN: Electron in ground state (1s orbital, spin ↓)
  - Center: electron nucleus distance = 0.53 Å
  - Magnetic moment: aligned with external field or random
  - Energy: baseline (ground state)

MOVE₁: Absorb photon (excitation)
  - Energy quantum: E = hν (frequency matches orbital gap)
  - Spin preparation: photon circularity aligns spin
  - Rabbit Hop: address electron from n=1 to n=2 orbital
    * Route receipt: electron | SHIFT_THEN_DOUBLE(1+1) | ±1 wrapper
    * ±1 wrapper = spin-up / spin-down configuration
  - Result: electron elevated to 2s or 2p orbital, spin aligned

HOLD: Resonance state (metastable)
  - Electron locked in 2p orbital
  - Spin locked to photon polarization
  - Magnetic moment now creates local field reorganization
  - This magnetic alignment STABILIZES the path choice
  - Duration: coherence time (nanoseconds typically)

MOVE₂: Phase coherence verification
  - Photon field couples to electron spin magnetic moment
  - Coherence check: does electron stay in 2p state?
  - If yes: path confirmed
  - If no: decay initiated

BREAK: Photon emission (relaxation)
  - Electron returns to 1s orbital
  - Releases photon with E = hν
  - Spin decouples from driving field
  - Energy released back to field

REPEAT: Ground state becomes reference for next excitation
  - Electron-nucleus system is now center for next BEGIN
  - Magnetic moment returns to aligned/unaligned equilibrium
  - Next photon can excite again
```

**Key Physics:** The HOLD phase is stabilized by magnetic locking. Without spin-orbit coupling (magnetism organizing the lattice), the electron would not stay in the excited state. Magnetism is what makes the path a stable choice.

```
electron | SHIFT_THEN_DOUBLE(n + K) | ±spin
```
