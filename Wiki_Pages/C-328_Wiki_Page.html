C-328: Common-Mode Field Cancellation

S_i = S_common + s_i' S_j = S_common + s_j' d-double-dot_ij = s_i' - s_j' (S_common cancels exactly)

A background acceleration shared by both bodies cannot show up in their relative motion, however large it is — the orbital-mechanics analogue of a differential amplifier's common-mode rejection (F-619).
