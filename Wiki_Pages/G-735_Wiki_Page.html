G-735: Prediction-Difference Gate

A relational mechanism counts as new physics only if its G-734 control-run comparison shows either (a) a measurable departure matching an observation the standard model reproduces worse, or (b) an equally accurate but genuinely simpler derivation. Matching the standard result via an equal-or-more-complex derivation is a consistency check passed, not a discovery.
