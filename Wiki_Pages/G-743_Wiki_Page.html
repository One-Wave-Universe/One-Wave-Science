G-743: Claim/Metaphor/Test Separation

Every new physical idea must separate: (1) mathematical definition, (2) physical hypothesis, (3) intuitive analogy (explicitly flagged as analogy), and (4) falsification test. Never let (3) stand in for (1)/(2), never present (2) as validated without a real (4).

Worked example: A-126 Finite Wake Assimilation's "wake"/"assimilation" language is analogy (3); its future full node must state the propagation math (1) and test (4) separately.
