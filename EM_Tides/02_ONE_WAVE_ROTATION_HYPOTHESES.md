# One-Wave Rotational Field Hypotheses

## Proposed premise
A magnetic field is hypothesized to be an effective large-scale description of transverse, rotational, phase-circulating organization in a single underlying propagation Field. This is not established physics.

## Required recovery limit
At accessible scales, the model must reproduce Maxwell-Lorentz phenomenology to experimental precision. A schematic underlying state may use a wave variable \(\Psi\), a field displacement \(u\), and a rotational or phase variable \(\theta\):

\[\partial_t^2\Psi=c_{\mathcal F}^2\nabla_{\mathcal F}^2\Psi+\mathcal N[\Psi,u,\theta]-\Gamma\partial_t\Psi.\]

The work is not complete until a defined mapping produces measurable effective fields:

\[\mathbf{E}_{\rm eff}=\mathcal E[\Psi,u,\theta],\qquad \mathbf{B}_{\rm eff}=\mathcal B[\Psi,u,\theta].\]

## Tidal proposal
A tide may be represented as a phase-lagged response of a bound, rotating, dissipative Field-body to an external displacement gradient:

\[\ddot u+2\gamma\dot u+\omega_0^2u=F_{\rm tidal}(t).\]

The One-Wave model must derive a torque \(\tau_{\rm OW}\) with the same measured sign reversal, distance dependence, resonance behavior, and energy budget as the standard tidal limit.

## Prohibited shortcuts
- Do not call magnetism gravity without a quantitative coupling and controls.
- Do not treat visual similarity between vortices, field lines, or spirals as causal evidence.
- Do not claim excess force without excluding magnetic pickup, electrostatics, thermal expansion, vibration, acoustic coupling, recoil, ground loops, and sensor drift.
- Do not use free-energy language: every effect must identify source work, stored field energy, mechanical work, heat, and radiation.

## Candidate coupling ledger
An additional electromagnetic-geometry coupling can be parameterized only as a hypothesis:

\[g_{\mu\nu}^{\rm eff}=g_{\mu\nu}^{(0)}+h_{\mu\nu}^{\rm mass}+\lambda_{\rm EM}h_{\mu\nu}^{\rm EM}+\lambda_{\rm rot}h_{\mu\nu}^{\rm rot}.\]

The parameters \(\lambda_{\rm EM}\) and \(\lambda_{\rm rot}\) must be constrained by existing null results and must predict a distinct measurable signal before being treated as physical.
