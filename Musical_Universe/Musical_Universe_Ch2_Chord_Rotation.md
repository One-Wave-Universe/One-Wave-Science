## Chord Rotation

Chord Rotation re-centers a chord on its chosen root. The root becomes `0`, and every other chord tone receives a mirrored route coordinate from Chapter 1.

The output is the complete coordinate set. It is not automatically a compression side and an expression side.

## Exact Coordinate Examples

Using root-relative pitch classes:

```text
Major triad      {0, +4, -5}
Minor triad      {0, +3, -5}
Power chord      {0, -5}
Augmented triad  {0, +4, -4}
Diminished triad {0, +3, ±6}
```

These values are coordinate facts under the selected sign convention. Their One-Wave meanings remain subject to testing.

## Important Cases

### Major and Minor

Major and minor share the root and fifth coordinate. Their third differs by one semitone:

```text
Major: {0, +4, -5}
Minor: {0, +3, -5}
```

This does not prove that one side is compressive or expressive.

### Power Chord

```text
{0, -5}
```

A power chord contains the root and fifth but no third. Therefore it does not select major or minor quality by itself.

Whether it should be interpreted as a reinforced boundary is an open Yellow question.

### Augmented

```text
{0, +4, -4}
```

The two non-root tones occupy equal-magnitude mirrored coordinates. Calling this a symmetric coordinate shape is exact. Any claim about a symmetric physical Field remains Yellow.

### Diminished

```text
{0, +3, ±6}
```

The tritone lands at the shared Mirror position. It must not be counted as merely `+6` or assigned automatically to one route.

The possibility that diminished harmony can resolve through more than one direction is an open Yellow question, not a locked mechanism.

## Retired Oscillation-Window Formula

The former representation

a two-value compression-side / expression-side pair

is retired because sign does not define operation, `±6` is shared, a dyad has no required second side, and extended chords lose information when collapsed to two extrema.

## Yellow Audit

- Chord spelling and coordinates are exact within the chosen tuning and root.
- Coordinate shape does not by itself establish force, tension, phase, or perceptual direction.
- Voicing, inversion, octave, amplitude, timing, and phase must be retained in later physical tests.
