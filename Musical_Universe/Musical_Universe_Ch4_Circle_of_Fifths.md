## Gray — Standard Circle of Fifths

Repeated perfect-fifth movement visits all twelve pitch classes before returning to the starting class.

Using pitch-class arithmetic:

`R_(n+1) = R_n + 7 mod 12`

Since `+7 mod 12 = -5`, the same traversal can be written:

`R_(n+1) = R_n - 5 mod 12`

The reverse traversal uses `+5 mod 12`, equivalent to descending fifths or ascending fourths.

## Relation to the Mirrored Music Clock

The chromatic clock and circle of fifths are not unrelated collections of twelve notes. The circle can be generated by repeatedly selecting a `±5` route from the current root and re-centering on the selected note.

```text
current root
→ select -5 or +5 route
→ make that pitch the next root
→ repeat
```

This is exact coordinate traversal.
