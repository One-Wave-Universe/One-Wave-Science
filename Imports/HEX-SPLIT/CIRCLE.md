# Circle of fifths overlay

Same twelve names as the hex flip. Not a second cosmology.

- `+7` weaves walls (leave this cell).
- `+5` reverse weave.
- `+6` stays home and crosses the flip zero.
- `+1` diagnostic. If it behaves like `+7` on a device, the generator is a drawing.

Files: `circle_of_fifths.py`, `circle_of_fifths.html`, `clock.json`.

BUCKET may drum the walk. GRAV does not glue fifths to B-lobes until a hex print beats a square.
One of the three midlines is the live hallway. Fifths is a candidate walk, not a force.
