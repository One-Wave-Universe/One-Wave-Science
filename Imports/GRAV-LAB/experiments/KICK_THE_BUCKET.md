# Kick the bucket

A marble votes. A scale votes. A sheet votes.

## Gear

- kitchen scale (0.01 if you can)
- neodymium spheres and a ceramic twin of similar heft
- wooden ramp, marks every 5 cm
- phone slow-mo
- compass / phone field app
- a bucket

## A — ramp

1. Write the angle down.
2. Ceramic, 5 runs.
3. Neo, empty bench, 5 runs.
4. Fixed magnet under the midline. Both twins.
5. Flip the magnet.
6. Tilt the magnet 15°.

Ask: did the *face of the run* change, or only the shove from the magnet shape?
If you cannot tell those apart, you do not have a lock claim.

## B — scale

1. Neo on the scale. 20 readings.
2. Second magnet near. Distances. Both ways.
3. Ceramic twin the same way.
4. Tin can / shield. Repeat.

If the shift dies in the can, you saw alignment shove, not lock.

## C — sheet

Sphere on a magnetic sheet. Film whether a repulsion start becomes a circling sit.
Geometry demo. Log frame rate and distances.
