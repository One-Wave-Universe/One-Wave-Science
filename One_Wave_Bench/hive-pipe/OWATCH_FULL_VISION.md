# OWATCH Full Vision — Watched Science Workshop

**Branch:** `owatch-logic-kernel`  
**Status:** branch architecture contract; not merged to `main`  
**Law:** one repo, one wave, separate floors, receipts at the joins.

## Floors

```text
CANON          Nodes/ + I-06 + books     OWATCH fail-closed
LOGIC          owatch-logic-kernel       derived / HOLD / prune

BUILD          cell / hardware           separate build tree when it exists
```

These floors must not flatten into each other.

The target is not "the graph wakes up." The target is a **watched science workshop** where a claim may be wrong, a derived relation may be retracted without rewriting source, a bench may fail publicly, and a digital twin never gets promoted into the body it imitates.
