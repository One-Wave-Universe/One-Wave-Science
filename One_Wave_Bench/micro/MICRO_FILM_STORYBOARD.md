# Micro film storyboard (art, not a derivation)

Shot 1 — black, one Point. Hex grows from it. Six pyramids. Sphere just a bound.
Shot 2 — knot inside, pale shell around it, weave on the skin, a seam that is the Mirror.
Shot 3 — shove. Four pressures at once. The seam brightens. Not a bouncing particle.
Shot 4 — basin flip. Same hold, inverted orientation. Outgoing modes as light, not letters.
Shot 5 — pull back: cell of that hold, then chip, then two Rubiks. Do not title it Higgs.
