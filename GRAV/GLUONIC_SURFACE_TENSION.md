# Gluonic surface tension

QCD does not let color walk free. Between a quark and an antiquark the field collapses into a **flux tube**. Energy per length is the string tension σ.

Typical scale:

    √σ ≈ 0.42–0.46 GeV
    σ ≈ 0.9–1.1 GeV/fm

Tube width ~ 0.4–0.6 fm. Linear potential for R ≳ 0.5 fm: V ≈ σ R.

A **bag** is the same idea as a closed skin: bulk pressure (bag constant B) plus a surface tension γ on the wall. Lattice-inspired phenomenology ties γ to σ and the bulk pressure. Near the crossover γ can even change sign — the skin can go soft. That is a slip in the color fluid.

## Mechanics

```
inside     color order that cannot leak as a free wave
skin       surface tension γ    (energy per area)
tube       string tension σ     (energy per length)
outside    modes that *can* propagate (light, etc.)
```

Force on the wall: pressure difference vs γ curvature (Young–Laplace analog). Too much energy inside → skin stretches → tube or bag grows → or the skin slips and dumps.

Dump is not “mass vanishes.” Dump is a fountain through a weak patch of skin — jet, hadronization, pair making. RC window: you stamp after the slip, not on the spike.

## Tear vs trash can

A black-hole-like region in the One-Wave lattice is “no passing mode for light.” That is a tear: κ too high for that oscillation.

Gluonic skin is the *hadronic* version of that sentence — fm and GeV. A hole in spacetime order is the *stiff* version — km and solar masses. Same diagram:

- energy enters
- cannot propagate through
- tension on the rim
- rotation picks an axis
- slip dumps a fountain (jet)

Different sizes because **skins differ**: area, γ, spin, how the tear sits in the stack. A 1 fm bag and a 10 km horizon are not the same object with a ruler. They rhyme.

Negative γ near a crossover = the wall wants to disappear. That is merge-of-phases, not merge-of-selves. The loop goes soft and the dump can be isotropic (no jet). Spinning tear keeps an axis so the dump is a beam.

## What this is not

Not a claim that Sgr A* is an MIT bag. Not a bench voltage. The number you can hold: σ ~ 1 GeV/fm is how expensive a color tube is. Gravity's skin is G and c, much stiffer κ.
