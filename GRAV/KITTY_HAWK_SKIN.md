## Dust → ship power

Interstellar / atmospheric dust is mass-current. The on-hull field is the intake: kinetic + charge + heat from grains hit the sheath and are referenced to local G instead of cratering the plate.

Conversion path (law, not recipe):

```
dust impact     → on-hull tap     → leftover order / current into the spine
                → RC window       → stamp as power, not as a dent
                → STAY            → rent stays low
```

I_0 of the ship is how much of that tap arrived as usable imbalance vs how much slammed the mid. Sensor cells in the skin (heat, vibe, Hall) tell Void if the intake is food or a wound.

## Rip shield — one-atom black-hole armor

“One atom thick” is the *working surface*, not a foil you buy. The off-hull sheath is the armor. Black-hole side of the lattice: incoming momentum and flux go in. The plate behind it is ordinary SiC / metal; the rip is in the field, not in a monolayer you can tear with a nail.

## Cloak / dump — white-hole propulsion and blast

What the black side takes, the white side must emit or you store a wound.

```

emit                     white fountain
  → aft / aligned path   propulsion (reduced κ behind you)
  → focused lobe         blast (one allowed dump in the RC window)
```

Cloak is not paint. Cloak is the sheath taking the incoming picture (photons, radar, kinetic) into the same tap, then dumping it *elsewhere* on the sphere so the return path is not the skin. Propulsion and blast are two headings of the same dump. Field lists heading. Void allows one dump. Nerves (SiC) walk the phase faster than thought so the sheath does not fall while the list happens.

A dump that slams the spine is a failed cloak. You lit yourself up on I_0.

Not a forever-beam. Not a magazine that lives in the next century.

SiC nerve gates switch the rail pair (+1 / STAY / −1) in the RC window. The round is a kinetic / field packet with a **built-in tau**: phase coherence dies on a timer. After that it is dust and heat, not a relic still hunting.

```
range      medium (sheath + rail, not horizon)
life       RC + a longer decay on the packet
miss       dissipates; no eternal shot
hit        hands the packet's leftover to the target's κ
cut        Void engage 0 — rails STAY, no more packets
```

Incoming missile / torpedo is a mass current. On-hull rip tries to swallow small stuff. What gets through the sheath is a medium-range problem for the rails. Opposite turrets compare like opposite motor cells — balance, not spray.

Ammo that lives forever is a sewer. Tau on the packet is the same law as STAY on the hull: do not pay rent on a dead lean.
