# Resistance equals time

Status: YELLOW lock for implementation and test. Does not replace SI clocks or claim a new constant of nature.

Date: 2026-09-18

## Lock

```
R ≡ κ
dt = R |ds| = κ |ds|
```

`docs/reference/MATH.md` already writes `dt = κ |ds|` and `ν/ν0 = κ0/κ`. Circuit resistance is the same job at the electrical octave: drop per current. Path resistance is drop per displacement. Resistance is the time to cross the patch, not a separate bill that later causes time.

## Consequences that stay attached

- Mass-cost: `m ∝ κ` of that patch.
- Gravity: `g ∝ −∇κ` (downhill in path cost).
- Redshift: same crest count paid against a stiffer `ds`.
- Wear: every paid `R|ds|` is a drive `u` into the hysteresis of integrity `Q`.
- Hop arithmetic is an address translator. It is not promoted into this force law.

## Anti-drift

- Do not treat `R = t` as a unit-cancellation slogan.
- Do not add a second clock beside `κ|ds|`.
- Do not silently expand space to manufacture extra `ds`.
- Equal destination of two paths does not mean equal time if the receipts (κ along each `ds`) differ.
